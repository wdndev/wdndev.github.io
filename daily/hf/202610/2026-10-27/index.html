1. WorldCast: World-Consistent Character Video Generation with Large-Angle Camera Control

Abstract:Precise camera control is essential for cinematic character video generation, yet existing methods struggle to follow large-angle trajectories with combined rotation, translation, and radius variation from arbitrary initial azimuths. Such viewpoint changes also reveal character regions unseen in the initial frame, causing geometric distortions, appearance degradation, and identity drift. To systematically study these coupled challenges, we introduce CLACT, a large-scale Character-centric dataset and benchmark covering diverse Large-Angle Camera Trajectories, with accurate camera geometry and multi-view character observations for training and evaluation. We further propose WorldCast, a framework for world-consistent character video generation under diverse large-angle camera motions. Specifically, we back-project the initial frame into a 3D point cloud using estimated depth, align the point-cloud scale with the target camera trajectory, and render it along the trajectory to obtain explicit geometric guidance and coverage masks. To complement the incomplete character information, we introduce Geometry-Aligned Memory Attention (GAMA). Using relative camera poses estimated jointly from the initial frame and multi-view character anchors, we express anchor and target-view geometry in a common coordinate frame. GAMA uses this geometric alignment to guide the retrieval and injection of view-relevant character features during generation. Evaluations across diverse large-angle camera trajectories demonstrate accurate trajectory following while preserving character geometry, appearance, and identity.

2. Continual Learning Exam: Benchmarking Long-Horizon RSI and Experiential Learning in LLM Agents

Abstract:Continual learning is the ability of LLM agents to improve over time by acquiring, refining, and applying knowledge from experience while retaining useful prior knowledge. It is essential for agents learning over long time horizons, yet current evaluations largely assess individual task performance rather than improvement through accumulated experience. We introduce Continual Learning Exam (CLE), a large-scale benchmark developed by 50+ domain experts to measure experience-driven improvement in realistic professional workflows. CLE comprises 120 human-authored tasks, each organised as a five-level ladder, yielding 600 levels spanning 89 professional roles across 12 occupational domains. Agents must pass each level to unlock the next, with later levels building on experience accumulated during earlier assignments. An evaluation agent assesses each submission against human-authored rubrics. We compare a single-submission setting with one that provides diagnostic feedback after failure and allows one revision. Across 17 harness-model configurations using frontier LLMs, feedback and revision yield a median completion gain of 5.3 percentage points. Yet the best-performing system clears only 47.7% of levels and completes all five levels in just 23.3% of tasks, highlighting the difficulty of sustained learning from experience for existing frontier LLMs. As such, the dataset can also be used to post-train LLMs to strengthen their continual learning capabilities.

环境状态 X 包含工作区文件与应用记录，动作 A 包含文件操作、API 调用与提交，观测 O 包含工具返回与评分反馈，奖励 R 在提交动作时根据评分结果给出 1 （通过）或 0 （失败）。 —- ### 5. 建立基于人工评分标准的自动化评估体系 为保证评估的客观性与可审计性，CLE 构建了独立于被测代理的**多轮评估代理（evaluation agent）**： - **16,125 条人工编写的评分标准（rubric criteria）**，平均每级 26.9 项； - 每项标准均指定**单一、明确的预期结果**及所需验证证据； - 评估代理在隔离的 Docker 环境中，使用工具从提交产物与环境状态中提取证据，对照人工设定的目标进行逐条判定； - 仅当某级别的**所有评分项均满足**时，该级别才视为通过。 该机制实现了对人类专业判断的高保真对齐：在验证样本上，评估代理与人类标注的**单项一致率达到 96.86%**，且三次重复运行的一致性达 99.46%。 —- ### 6. 通过大规模实验定位当前能力边界 论文在上述框架下评估了 **17 种 harness–model 配置**（涵盖 4 种代理框架与 11 种前沿骨干模型），在模型参数冻结的条件下进行全量测试。实验设计的关键在于： - 所有配置按**固定顺序**串行执行全部 120 个任务阶梯； - 代理框架的持久状态（记忆、技能、反思记录等）**跨任务保留**，但模型权重不变； - 通过对比 Trial 1 与 Trial 2 的结果，测量**经验重用与反馈修正**带来的改进空间。 实验结果揭示了现有系统的持续学习瓶颈：即使是最优配置（Claude Code / Opus 5），在 Trial 2 中也仅完成 **47.67%** 的级别，全部五级通关率仅为 **23.33%**；中位系统从反馈中获得的完成率提升为 **5.3 个百分点**。这些数据证明 CLE 尚未被现有方法饱和，从而为后续算法改进提供了明确的优化目标与测量工具。 —- ### 总结 简言之，该论文通过**“递进式任务阶梯 + 状态化环境 + 受控反馈协议 + 可审计的自动化评分”**四位一体的设计，将 LLM 代理的持续学习能力从不可观测的“黑箱”转化为**可量化、可对比、可复现**的评估指标，从而系统性地解决了该领域缺乏针对性基准的问题。 Q4: 论文做了哪些实验？ 论文在第 4 节及附录 F 中报告了系统性实验，围绕 **17 种 harness–model 配置**在 CLE 基准上的持续学习表现展开。实验可归纳为**主实验**、**附加分析**与**模型代际对比**三个层面。 —- ### 1. 实验设置 #### 1.1 系统配置 共评估 **17 个配置**，涵盖 4 个代理框架（harness）与 11 个骨干模型： - **Claude Code**

gt sim ωθ(·|h_t, a_t) ∈ ∪ G
其中 表示静默（无陷阱）， G 为自然语言指导空间。智能体最终执行的动作由以下规则确定：
a_t = a_t, & if g_t = , sim π(·|h_t, a_t, g_t), & otherwise.
这一机制同时回答了两个关键设计问题： - **何时干预**：仅在预见潜在陷阱时开口，避免对已在正确轨迹上的智能体造成不必要的干扰和 API 调用开销。 - **输出什么**：输出的是从当前动作推演至**下一个里程碑（subgoal）** 的多步轨迹预测，而非单步观察或无界的全程轨迹。该预测聚焦于“该动作将导致何种失败或无效循环”，从而提供最具信息量的纠正信号。 —- ### 2. 阶段一：基于合成陷阱数据的监督微调（SFT） 该阶段旨在教会 PAWM 识别陷阱位置并生成相应的预警语言。 #### 2.1 轨迹收集与对比标注 首先，使用黑盒 LLM（Claude Sonnet 4.5）在训练集上重复采样任务轨迹（温度设为 1.0），保留成功与失败轨迹。随后，利用另一个 LLM 作为标注器，通过以下策略构造监督数据： - **对比标注（Contrastive Labeling）**：对同一任务的成功与失败轨迹进行对比，定位失败轨迹中**最早**偏离成功路径的动作（`fail_start_step`），并预测从该动作起将引发的失败后果，生成 g_t 。若失败仅由环境噪声（如步数限制、非确定性事件）导致，则标记为无陷阱。 - **成功轨迹的残余陷阱标注**：对仅有成功轨迹的任务，识别其中次优但非致命的绕路（detour），避免模型退化成全静默。 - **失败轨迹的引导成功**：对仅有失败轨迹的任务，注入人工指导重新运行以获得成功轨迹，再进行对比标注。 所有标注被约束为仅基于当前步可见信息的抽象描述，避免未来信息泄露。最终得到数据集 D = (h_t, a_t, g_t) 。 #### 2.2 训练与类别平衡 SFT 通过最小化负对数似然进行：

L(SFT)(θ) = -E((ht,a)_t,g_t)sim D [ log ωθ(gt|h_t, a_t) ]

8. Principia: Test-Time Hypotheses to Principle Construction for Scientific Discovery

Abstract:Scientific discovery requires identifying the scientific principles underlying observed phenomena to guide further investigation. As these empirical principles are often unknown in complex discovery processes, they are constructed via a hypotheses-to-principle pipeline to obtain recurring causalities from progressively accumulated observations during test-time discovery. However, constructing this knowledge requires substantial domain expertise, time, and effort, making this translation stage a bottleneck for scientific discovery. To address this limitation, we propose Principia, an LLM-based framework that constructs and continually refines scientific principles at test time. Drawing on how human researchers develop principles, Principia forms comparative causal hypotheses from related inputs and their observed outcomes, then synthesizes recurring relationships across these hypotheses into inductive principles. By applying the constructed hypotheses and inductive principles to the task, it obtains new observations, revises the hypotheses used based on these new observations, and reconstructs principles from the updated hypotheses, allowing scientific knowledge to continuously evolve throughout test-time discovery. Across benchmarks spanning molecular optimization, protein engineering, gene perturbation, and molecular property prediction, Principia outperforms baselines relying on internal knowledge, external sources, or experience summaries, while generating principles that are plausible and consistent with the scientific literature, demonstrating its effectiveness in constructing and refining task-specific hypotheses and principles at test time for scientific discovery.
