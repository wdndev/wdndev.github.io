1. Motion Mamba 2.0: Autoregressive Diffusion Mamba for Long-Horizon Humanoid Control

Abstract:Long-horizon, real-time controllable motion generation is essential for interactive animation and humanoid robotics, where an agent must respond to changing instructions and spatial goals while moving. Existing autoregressive diffusion methods generate motion incrementally, but bounded attention caches limit access to distant history, while repeated denoising introduces substantial inference overhead. We present Motion Mamba 2.0, a Mamba-based root-body diffusion architecture that integrates local attention, resumable recurrent states, and persistent content memory (PCM) for long-horizon controllable motion generation. PCM transforms compressed motion history from Mamba recurrent states, together with state changes and text-conditioned features, into dynamically updated content slots accessible to local attention. To enable efficient inference, we further develop an execution scheme tailored to this architecture, combining CUDA Graph replay, fused kernels, and execution-flow simplification while preserving the canonical history update. Evaluations on retargeted HiPHI demonstrate accurate terminal control over rollouts of up to 39.52 s, while short-horizon comparisons on BONES Seed and retargeted HumanML3D show that the model also retains strong spatial control and text-motion matching. On an RTX 4090, the optimized execution reduces median complete-window latency from 1685.35 to 276.14 ms, achieving a median paired speedup of 6.138$\times$ while preserving identical outputs and committed states in the tested rollouts. Moreover, we conduct extensive real-world evaluation on the Unitree G1, demonstrating sequential text-guided action composition, expressive motion, and interactive locomotion through keyboard and mouse control.

2. 3AGameFactory-Motion: Agentic Rigging and Reliable Complex Motion Generation for Diverse 3D Assets

Abstract:Animating arbitrary 3D assets from instructions requires both structural inference and motion control, yet existing motion benchmarks mostly cover conventional human or animal skeletons with simple instructions. We introduce DivDynBench, which evaluates rigging and motion generation across six morphology categories, with instructions requiring multi-phase part coordination and explicit timing, angle, repetition, and trajectory constraints. With large language models, we systematically compare three common paradigms: specialized motion generation, video generation followed by motion recovery, and direct agent-driven generation. We analyze their control capabilities, cross-morphology adaptability, and generation costs. Guided by this comparison, we propose an Agentic Motion Framework in which an agent writes compact parametric skeleton and motion programs, and a deterministic executor performs rigging, kinematic solving, and geometric correction. Compact parameter control reduces generation overhead and improves rigging reliability and motion-control accuracy, providing practical guidance for deploying complex 3D motion generation.

3. AutoPILOT: A Visual Agent Harness for Real-Time Gameplay

Abstract:Multimodal foundation models can play video games well when the game waits for them, but often fail when it keeps running. We find that much of this gap lies in the harness, which decides what the model sees, what it remembers, and how its outputs become actions over time. Existing harnesses such as VISTA improve the first two, yet turn each model call into at most one action, so the agent acts only as often as its model thinks. We introduce AutoPILOT, which changes the third: in each model call, the model can send a primitive action, start an open-loop macro, or write a closed-loop controller that keeps playing from the screen without further model calls until it hands control back. To evaluate agents in real-time games, we build AFK-Bench, 120 diverse browser games grouped by how they respond to inaction and playable in real time or with the clock paused. With GPT-6.1 Sol under the same budgets, AutoPILOT reaches 68.6% real-time success against 38.3% for VISTA, and on GameWorld it keeps 80.0% in real time while VISTA drops from 84.1% to 42.9%. Its gains hold across models, and it reaches VISTA’s final success rate at a fifth of its API cost. Our results suggest that how a harness turns model outputs into actions over time is a central yet underexplored part of agent design, and we hope AutoPILOT and AFK-Bench will serve as a foundation for agents in a world that does not wait.

4. Plan4D: Generative Plannable 4D Worlds

Abstract:Most world models for future prediction prioritize aestetic quality of generated videos, but offer only weak physical grounding and limited geometric consistency over time, due to the lack of explicit 4D modeling. In this paper, we introduce plan4d, a possible next milestone towards building a generative plannable 4D world model that predicts future RGB videos together with dense 4D geometry, including depth, cameras, point clouds, and scene flow from an observed scene context and text-specified actions. Since training data with paired actions and dense 4D annotations is inherently scarce, plan4d leverages the complementary priors of foundational video generators and 3D reconstructors. Specifically, we propose a model-stitching architecture that couples Depth Anything 3 with LongCat-Video and augments it with an auxiliary DPT head for scene-flow prediction. The resulting model allows us to plan and generate complex, long-horizon, and fine-grained actions in a dynamic 3D scenes, while substantially improving both visual fidelity and geometric accuracy of the generated future videos. Extensive experiments on both synthetic and real-world datasets demonstrate that plan4d achieves state-of-the-art performance in generative 4D modeling of environmental reactions. Q: 这篇论文试图解决什么问题？ 这篇论文旨在解决\*\*生成式可规划4D世界建模\*\*的问题。具体而言，其核心目标与面临的挑战可归纳如下： ### 1. 现有方法的局限性 当前的世界模型（World Models）在未来预测任务中存在显著的\*\*模态割裂\*\*与\*\*几何不一致性\*\*： - \*\*视频生成模型\*\*（如 UniSim、Genie、NWM 等）主要将未来预测视为RGB帧的合成任务，虽能生成视觉上逼真的视频，但缺乏显式的4D（3D空间+时间）物理建模，导致时序几何一致性差。 - \*\*3D重建模型\*\*虽能恢复显式几何结构，但大多以重建为中心且面向静态场景。 - \*\*现有4D框架\*\*只能从已观测的帧中推断运动，无法基于文本描述的动作生成条件化的未来动态4D世界。 因此，现有方法均无法在单一前馈模型中联合生成\*\*外观、几何、相机参数与动作条件化未来动态\*\*且保持时序一致。 ### 2. 提出的新范式：Generative Plannable 4D Worlds 论文引入了一个新的任务范式：给定\*\*观察到的场景上下文\*\* O = I_i_(i=1)^M 和\*\*文本描述的动作序列\*\* A ，模型需联合预测未来的RGB视频 I_t 及其对应的密集4D几何表示：

其中 R(t to 0) 表示从第 t 帧相机基系到首帧基系的旋转。 这一设计使得冻结的 DA3 不仅能重建静态几何，还能读出稠密前向 3D 运动，且论文附录中的分析表明 DA3 的 token 本身已编码跨帧运动线索。 —- ### 2. 潜在空间到几何的模型缝合（Latent-to-Geometry Stitching） 为了将视频生成器的潜在状态与 DA3 的几何 token 空间对接，PLAN4D 设计了一个**可学习的缝合模块** Sθ ： - 给定视频帧 I(1:T) ，先通过冻结的 **Wan 2.1 VAE** 编码为潜在变量 z(1:T)^0 = E(vae)(I(1:T)) 。 - 对潜在变量进行时空上采样 z(1:T)^0 = Up((s_t, s_h, s_w))(z(1:T)^0) ，以匹配 DA3 的 patch 网格分辨率。 - 缝合模块 S_θ （实例化为紧凑的 3D 卷积）将上采样后的潜在张量映射到选定的 DA3 编码器块 k^star 的通道维度：

h^((k^star)) = Sθ!(z(1:T)^0)
**注入层的选择**并非启发式决定。论文通过正则化线性拟合在多个候选块上求解：
(k^star, θ0) = argmin(k,θ) ∑i | Sθ!(z_(1:T)^(0,(i))) - g_i^((k)) |_2^2 + λ |θ|_2^2
其中 g_i^((k)) 是冻结 DA3 在第 k 块的特征网格。该过程选出从视频 VAE 潜在空间**线性可达**的最优几何特征层，并提供稳定的初始化 θ_0 。 缝合后，DA3 的原始 RGB patch 嵌入与 k^star 之前的编码器块被绕过；投影后的潜在 token 与 DA3 的特殊 token 及位置编码结合，经剩余编码器块和原生密集头部处理，直接输出深度、相机射线、置信度图和场景流。 —- ### 3. 缝合解码器的微调与校准 缝合模块仅提供初始化，需进一步微调以适应新的潜在输入分布并保证几何精度。PLAN4D 采用**混合训练策略**，在严格冻结视频扩散 Transformer 的前提下，仅训练缝合解码器： - **教师-学生蒸馏**：冻结的 DA3（教师）以真实 RGB 视频为输入，缝合解码器（学生）以相同片段的 Wan VAE 潜在为输入，二者输出的深度、射线、置信度、相机参数和场景流进行对齐。 - **真值监督**：对提供 3D 标注的数据集，直接使用深度、射线、相机、点云和场景流的真值作为回归目标。 几何损失函数为：

L(geo) = λ_D L(depth) + λR L(ray) + λC L(camera) + λM L(motion) + λT L(temporal)
该阶段将 DA3 解码器转化为针对 Wan 潜在空间的**鲁棒几何读出机制**。 —- ### 4. 视频与4D几何的联合训练 缝合解码器微调完成后被**冻结**，并作为**可微几何评判器（differentiable geometry critic）** 用于监督视频扩散模型（LongCat-Video）。 - 视频模型遵循标准的**潜在流匹配（latent flow matching）**目标。对噪声潜在 z(1:T)^τ 、扩散时间 τ 和条件 c （文本动作嵌入、上下文帧潜在及掩码），DiT 预测速度场 vφ(z_(1:T)^τ, τ, c) ，并转换为干净潜在估计：

z(1:T)^0 = z(1:T)^τ - στ , vφ(z_(1:T)^τ, τ, c)

  • 该 z(1:T)^0 被直接送入**冻结的缝合解码器** D(stitch) ，解码出对应的 4D 几何。 - 联合目标函数为：
    L(joint) = L(FM) + λ(geo) , L(geo)!(D(stitch)(z(1:T)^0))
    在联合优化阶段，仅通过教师-学生蒸馏对动作视频进行几何监督（因 Action100M 等大规模动作数据集缺乏 4D 标注）。对于图像到视频生成，首帧作为条件前缀，扩散损失可屏蔽该前缀，而几何损失仅作用于生成的后缀帧。 —- ### 推理阶段 在推理时，PLAN4D 执行**单次前向传播**： - 视频扩散模型从观测上下文和动作文本中生成一条共享的潜在轨迹； - **Wan VAE 解码器**将其还原为 RGB 视频； - **缝合的 DA3 解码器**从同一潜在轨迹中同步读出深度图、相机射线、相机参数、点云和场景流。 通过这种方式，PLAN4D 将预训练视频生成器转化为一个动作条件化的 4D 世界模型，在不依赖大规模动作-4D 配对标注数据的情况下，实现了视觉保真度与几何准确性的统一。 Q4: 论文做了哪些实验？ 论文在合成与真实世界数据集上开展了系统性实验，沿三条主线评估生成式可规划4D世界模型的性能：**动态4D几何估计**、**缝合解码器的几何保持能力**，以及**完整的动作条件化联合4D生成**。以下是实验内容的详细总结： —- ### 1. 实验设置与协议 #### 1.1 三阶段训练配置 - **Stage 1（场景流头训练）**：在 PointOdyssey、Dynamic Replica、BlinkVision、Virtual KITTI 2、OmniWorld-Game 上训练新增的场景流分支，冻结 DA3 主干。 - **Stage 2（缝合解码器微调）**：先拟合潜在-几何缝合模块，再使用混合监督（真值标注 + 教师-学生蒸馏）微调缝合后的 DA3 解码器，增加 ScanNet++ 和 MVS-Synth 用于几何监督。 - **Stage 3（联合训练）**：冻结缝合解码器作为可微几何评判器，在 Action100M 子集上训练视频扩散 Transformer（LongCat-Video），使用 LoRA 进行参数高效微调。 #### 1.2 评估数据集 实验在以下五类基准上进行： - **场景流评估**：MOVi-F、Dynamic Replica、PointOdyssey（81帧片段）。 - **几何保留评估**：ETH3D、ScanNet++、Dynamic Replica、Dynamic-RE10K、MOVi-F、RobbySimVal。 - **联合4D生成评估**：RLBench、HOIGen-1M、Dynamic Replica、PointOdyssey，以及野外日常交互数据集 **Epic-Kitchens-100**（100个5秒片段）。 #### 1.3 对比基线 - **场景流**：TraceAnything、Any4D、MotionCrafter、V-DPM、Zero-MSF+GT。 - **几何保留**：VGGT、DA3（RGB输入）、VIST3A-Stitched、Gen3R-VAE。 - **联合生成**：TesserAct、Gen3R、VIST3A。 —- ### 2. 主要实验结果 #### 2.1 场景流估计 验证冻结 DA3 的几何先验是否足以支撑动态4D运动预测。 - **指标**：端点误差（EPE）与 APD(0.05) （误差小于 0.05 的比例）。 - **结果**： - RGB 基础的 DA3 变体在真实动态基准（Dynamic Replica、PointOdyssey）上表现强劲，表明冻结 DA3 token 已编码可靠的跨帧运动线索。 - 缝合变体（Stitched）在合成基准 MOVi-F 上取得最佳性能（EPE 13.10，APD 93.32），验证了潜在-几何接口的有效性。 #### 2.2 缝合几何解码器的几何保持能力 评估用 Wan 视频潜在替代 RGB 输入后，DA3 的深度、相机和重建行为是否得以保留。 - **指标**： - 重建：精度（Acc.）、完整性（Comp.）、F-score（ETH3D、ScanNet++、Dynamic Replica）。 - 相机：RRA@ 5^circ 、AUC30、AUC15（Dynamic Replica、Dynamic-RE10K）。 - 深度： δ_1 （阈值精度）、AbsRel（MOVi-F、RobbySimVal）。 - **结果**： - 缝合解码器在所有几何族上均比 VIST3A-Stitched 和 Gen3R-VAE 更接近原始 RGB 基础的 DA3 与 VGGT。 - 在 Dynamic Replica 上取得最佳完整性（0.0658），在 Dynamic-RE10K 上相机 AUC30（0.5350）与 AUC15（0.4124）均超过 DA3 基线。 #### 2.3 联合 4D 世界生成 测试单一模型是否能同时生成视觉逼真的视频与物理一致的密集4D几何。 - **视频质量指标**：主题一致性（SC）、背景一致性（BC）、美学质量（AQ）、图像质量（IQ）、运动平滑度（MS）、视频-字幕相似度（Cap.Sim）。 - **几何与相机指标**：深度 AbsRel、 δ_1 、Chamfer-L1、相机相对旋转误差（RPE-R）、RRA@ 5^circ / 10^circ 。 - **结果**（关键结论）： - **视频质量**：PLAN4D 在所有五个数据集上取得最佳主题一致性；在 Epic-Kitchens-100 和 PointOdyssey 上取得最佳字幕相似度。 - **深度与重建**：在全部五个数据集上取得最低 AbsRel；在 Epic-Kitchens-100、Dynamic Replica、PointOdyssey 和 RLBench 上 Chamfer-L1 最低。 - **相机一致性**：在 HoiGen、Dynamic Replica、PointOdyssey 和 Epic-Kitchens-100 上取得最佳 RRA@ 5^circ ，相对旋转误差显著低于 Gen3R 与 VIST3A。 —- ### 3. 消融研究 #### 3.1 联合训练目标消融 在 HoiGen 和 Dynamic Replica 上对比三种训练目标： - **Only-RGB-Loss**：仅优化潜在流匹配损失 L(FM) 。 - **Only-Geo-Loss**：仅优化几何损失 λ(geo)L(geo) 。 - **Ours (Full)**：联合优化两者。 - **结论**： - 仅 RGB 损失保留强视觉一致性，但深度与相机精度较差。 - 仅几何损失在 HoiGen 上深度最优，但牺牲视觉一致性，且在 Dynamic Replica 上全面劣化。 - **完整目标**在视觉一致性（SC、BC）与相机估计上均取得最佳，证明两者互补。 #### 3.2 缝合适配器架构消融 在 OmniWorld-Game 上比较三种缝合模块设计： - **线性3D卷积**：纯局部投影。 - **残差瓶颈**：线性跳跃连接 + 非线性修正分支。 - **轴向注意力**：卷积词干 + 沿时间/空间轴的独立自注意力。 - **结论**：三种架构均倾向于选择最早的 DA3 编码器块作为注入点；线性3D卷积的拟合误差（MSE 0.268）已与更复杂的残差（0.254）和注意力（0.266）接近，支持采用极简设计。 #### 3.3 缝合解码器微调策略消融 在 ETH3D 和 Dynamic-RE10K 上评估不同微调策略： - **仅蒸馏**：重建精度显著退化（ETH3D Acc. 1.4276）。 - **蒸馏+特征损失**：引入特征对齐反而损害性能（ETH3D Acc. 0.5209）。 - **仅 GT 监督**：相机估计崩溃（AUC30 降至 0.3141）。 - **无场景流头**：几何性能下降。 - **LoRA 适配**：重建严重退化（ETH3D Acc. 2.6344）。 - **结论**：**混合监督（蒸馏 + GT）** 是保留 DA3 先验并纠正潜在输入分布偏移的最优策略。 —- ### 4. 补充与诊断实验（附录） #### 4.1 视频-仅生成 + 后处理重建对比 在 HoiGen 上对比级联基线： - **Type A（语义不相交）**：如厨房图像配”企鹅从冰山跃入海洋”。结果：100% rollout 通过**场景切换（Cut）**满足文本。 - **Type B（场景编辑）**：如要求将面团变为玻璃珠。结果：88% 通过**瞬时形变（Morph）**实现，12% 保持原场景合理延续。 - **结论**：模型通常优先遵循文本条件；几何解码器仍输出同步的深度与流，但在切换处可能出现物理不连续。 #### 4.3 更多定性结果 提供了涵盖细粒度手物交互（如挤压瓶挤酱）、人体运动（如瑜伽动作）、物理击打（如拳击沙袋）及多实体室内动态（如人、猴、牛同时移动）的 RGB、深度图与全局动态点云可视化。 Q5: 有什么可以进一步探索的点？ 基于该论文的框架与讨论，以下方向具有进一步探索的潜力： —- ### 1. 闭环具身智能体集成与规划 论文在 Limitations 中指出，PLAN4D 目前是一个**离线的几何感知世界模型**，尚未与显式动作策略结合以形成闭环的具身系统。未来的研究可着眼于： - 10. WeightCode: Installing New Capabilities into Neural Networks via Weight-Space Programming

Abstract:Neural networks encode learned capabilities in their weights, yet extending those capabilities with explicit task knowledge remains a fundamental challenge. Here we show that Weight-Space Programming (WSP), in which executable programs read, construct or modify a network’s weights directly, can install new capabilities within trained neural networks. WeightCode, a WSP framework, uses a frozen language model to write executable weight programs that install new computations into trained neural networks using task knowledge and their existing weights, without gradient-based training. Once installed, the new capabilities run independently of the language model. Given a grammar or eight demonstrations, weight programs enable previously unseen command compositions (0% to 99.8% exact match) and a new pickup action (0% to 98.4% success), while preserving the original command and navigation capabilities. On ImageNet, four weight programs achieve net gains of 8.84-17.62 points while preserving clean accuracy, outperforming the best of eight fine-tuning methods under matched examples, checks, and retention constraints. In a Vision Transformer, causal interventions show that installed noise-correction gates drive robustness gains, while their selective activation preserves clean accuracy. Rerun on five independently trained Vision Transformers without further language model calls, ten weight programs retain 83-110% of their source-network gains on average, whereas copying their fixed weight edits yields negligible gains. Six programs written for an already edited network add 5.23-14.50 points of contrast robustness while retaining the previously installed noise gain. Together, these results establish WSP as a new route to acquiring and accumulating capabilities in trained neural networks through reusable weight programs.

W’ = p(W, I), quad Delta W = p(W, I) - W
这种分离使得程序保留的是**构造过程**（如何测量、选择、写入），而非仅仅是最终的数值 Delta W 。 —- ### 2. 基于评估反馈的程序选择与保留规则 程序不是一次性写成的，而是通过**检查-反馈循环**筛选出来的： - **候选构造**：LLM 在单轮会话中提出至多 8 个候选权重修改，每个候选都是对网络内部计算（如特定 MLP 单元、注意力头、卷积核）的重写提议。 - **保留规则（Retention Rule）**：在检查集（check split）上评估每个候选，计算两个指标： - S(new) ：新能力相关输入中被修复的数量减去被破坏的数量； - S(old) ：旧能力相关输入上的同样净计数。 候选必须满足 S(old) ≥ -20 （例如，在 4,000 张干净检查图像上最多损失 0.5% 的准确率），且在所有满足保留规则的候选中选择 S(new) + S_(old) 最高者。若无一满足，网络保持不变。 这确保了新能力的安装以**显式约束旧能力损失**为前提。 —- ### 3. 无梯度、基于网络内部统计的直接权重构造 与微调不同，WeightCode **完全避免基于输出损失的梯度优化**。程序通过以下无梯度操作构造修改： - **权重与激活统计**：读取网络各层的均值、方差、协方差； - **矩阵分解**：对激活或权重进行 SVD、PCA，识别网络中已有的低维子空间或方向； - **前向传播与单元消融**：运行网络前向传播，测量各单元对特定输入的响应，定位在干净数据上”几乎空闲”的容量； - **闭式拟合**：基于测量到的偏移量直接设置权重值。 例如，在 ViT-Lite 的高斯噪声程序中，LLM 发现噪声主要沿特定方向偏移 patch token，于是将两个几乎不用于干净图像的 MLP 单元重写为**门控校正器**：

17. SAVIOR: Sample-efficient Adaptation of Vision-Language Models for OCR Representation

Abstract:Optical Character Recognition (OCR) pipelines and vision-language models exhibit high accuracy on standard OCR and document-understanding benchmarks, yet they systematically underperform on document patterns that are operationally critical in financial workflows. These patterns include vertical or rotated text, logo-embedded vendor names, fine-print regulatory clauses, degraded scans, and complex multi-column layouts. While underrepresented in public datasets, they constitute a substantial portion of real-world failure cases. We introduce SAVIOR, a sample-efficient data curation methodology that targets such high-impact failure scenarios to adapt vision-language models for robust financial OCR. We further propose PaIRS, a structure-aware evaluation metric that measures layout fidelity by comparing pairwise spatial relationships between tokens. Fine-tuning Qwen2.5-VL-Instruct with SAVIOR-Train achieves a word recall of 0.93. When the resulting layout-aware OCR output is used as context for document question answering, it attains an F1 score of 0.89, outperforming both open- and closed-source models such as GPT-4o, Mistral-OCR, PaddleOCR-VL, and DeepSeek-OCR.

18. Write, Evolve, Read: Streaming Cameras into a Persistent World State for 4D Occupancy Forecasting

Abstract:4D occupancy forecasting predicts the 3D semantic occupancy of a driving scene seconds ahead. Over a few seconds, the scene changes little while the vehicle moves, so most of the space ahead has already been seen. Sorting the future into unchanged, changed, and unseen space, we find that current camera-based world models fall short on the unchanged part: they fail to hold on to what they have already seen, rather than to forecast what will change. We therefore make what the model has seen its state. PersistWorld streams camera frames into a persistent 3D world state anchored in the scene, writing each frame once instead of rebuilding the state from past frames. The state is learned end to end with the forecast, and an evolver predicts it at any queried time along the ego trajectory. On Occ3D-nuScenes, PersistWorld raises the IoU of the best camera-based world model by 19 points at 1-3 s and by 18 to 24 points up to 8 s, while running more than twice as fast. 19. Distill the Correction, Not the Rewrite: Hindsight Twins for Self-Improving Text-to-Image Models

Abstract:Text-to-image models can learn from their own mistakes by distilling a self-teacher conditioned on a vision-language critic’s revised prompt. However, such self-teachers barely improve alignment, and both they and their hindsight-contrast variants absorb the critic’s unintended additions to the scene, such as lighting or scenery. We attribute this to two factors: (1) the prompt already specifies the desired fix, so a useful revision must go beyond the failed span, often rewriting the entire scene and adding details that overshadow the fix; (2) although the critic localizes the error to a single span, self-teachers condition on the rewrite, entangling the intended fix with unintended additions. Motivated by these insights, we propose Hindsight Twins (HinT), which fills the critic’s revision template once with the intended fix and once with the observed error and distills only their velocity difference, anchored at the user’s prompt. Because text encoders spread the edit of a single slot to all other tokens, HinT further aligns the twins’ token states outside the slot. Across Qwen-Image-2512, SD3.5-M, and FLUX.2-klein, HinT keeps the rate of critic-induced bias at the base-model level while matching or exceeding the alignment gains of prior self-distillation, and it keeps improving over twelve rounds of self-training where contrastive baselines decay.

中文摘要

摘要：文本到图像模型可以通过蒸馏一个受视觉-语言评论者修订的提示条件影响的自我教师，从自身的错误中学习。然而，这种自我教师对对齐的改进几乎微不足道，而且它们及其事后对比变体都会吸收评论者对场景的意外补充，例如光照或景物。我们将其归因于两个因素：（1）提示已明确指定所需的修正，因此有用的修订必须超越失败的片段，通常需要重写整个场景并添加掩盖修正的细节；（2）尽管评论者将错误定位到单个片段，自我教师却以重写作为条件，将预期修正与意外新增内容混合。基于这些洞察，我们提出了事后双体（Hindsight Twins, HinT），它在评论者的修订模板中一次填写预期修正，一次填写观察到的错误，并仅蒸馏它们的速度差异，同时以用户提示为锚点。由于文本编码器会将单个槽位的编辑扩展到所有其他标记，HinT进一步对齐双体在该槽位之外的标记状态。在Qwen-Image-2512、SD3.5-M和FLUX.2-klein上，HinT将评论者引入偏差的比例保持在基础模型的水平，同时匹配或超过先前自我蒸馏的对齐增益，并在自我训练的十二轮中持续改进，而对比基线则出现下降。

20. Typhoon OCR 2: From Thai Document Transcription to Multilingual, Multi-Task OCR

Abstract:Typhoon OCR 1.5 focused on reading Thai documents. However, in real-world workflows, users often need to extract specific information or preserve the structure of an entire page for further processing. Typhoon OCR 2 handles both tasks in a single model, with prompt-driven extraction and structured full-page parsing. Full-page parsing covers Thai, English, and Chinese. We release compact 2B and 0.8B models adapted from open document parsers through supervised fine-tuning on curated Thai and public multilingual data, followed by Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) with task rewards and a rule penalizing unfinished responses. Among evaluated open models, Typhoon OCR 2 2B scores highest on Thai MDPBench (80.7 versus 68.9 for Typhoon OCR 1.5) and on ThaiOCRBench (59.91 versus 40.45), which covers recognition, extraction, and document understanding. The 2B model remains close to its base on English olmOCR-bench (84.7 versus 84.4) and on OmniDocBench, which evaluates English and Chinese together (90.97 versus 91.03). On MDPBench, GRPO reduces the mean share of pages with repetition loops for the 2B model from 3.2% to 0.7%, with a smaller and statistically uncertain score gain for the 0.8B model. We release the model weights, Python package, and evaluation suite.
