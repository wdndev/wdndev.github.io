1. AdaTutoRank: Learning to Rerank Document Sets via Adaptive Tutoring Optimization for RAG and Deep Research

Abstract:Document rerankers determine what evidence reaches the downstream model in RAG and deep research, yet mainstream rerankers select by relevance matching, and individually relevant documents rarely constitute the complete, complementary, non-redundant set a complex information need demands. Prior work rewards a set by its aggregate rubric score, shifting the objective from ranking documents to composing sets. Yet that score is one scalar shared by every document in the set, so the supervision is sparse: a redundant document is rewarded with the rest whenever the set scores well, and a decisive one penalized with the rest whenever it does not; credit assignment leaves contributors indistinguishable from free riders. On-policy distillation could densify this supervision, but existing methods give every rollout the same fixed guidance, too prescriptive for strong rollouts and too abstract for weak ones. We therefore propose AdaTutoRank, a setwise reranker trained with Adaptive Tutoring Optimization (ATO) under a three-level hierarchy of nine rubric dimensions, which supplies silver labels for the cold start, rewards for reinforcement learning, and hints for distillation. ATO draws three hint forms of increasing specificity from the policy’s own frozen snapshot: the rubrics alone, a self-selector’s sibling-set chosen under rubrics, and a self-reflector’s reflection contrasting the rollout with that sibling-set; each rollout receives the form matched to its quality. Re-scoring that rollout under the hint-conditioned frozen teacher and the hint-free snapshot distills the hint’s effect into a token-level advantage that complements the group-relative outcome advantage. Across ten benchmarks spanning RAG, deep research, and setwise evaluation, AdaTutoRank attains the best overall performance while issuing fewer retrieval calls.

中文摘要

摘要：文档重排序器决定了哪些证据可以传递到RAG和深度研究中的下游模型，但主流重排序器是通过相关性匹配来选择文档的，而单个相关文档往往难以构成复杂信息需求所需的完整、互补且无冗余的集合。先前的工作通过其总评分来奖励一个集合，将目标从排序文档转向组合集合。然而，该评分是集合中每个文档共享的一个标量，因此监督信号是稀疏的：当集合得分良好时，冗余文档会与其他文档一起获得奖励，而关键文档当集合得分不佳时也会与其他文档一起受到惩罚；这样分配贡献，贡献者与搭便车者无法区分。策略内蒸馏可以增强这种监督，但现有方法对每次生成都给出相同的固定指导，对表现良好的生成过于限制，对表现较差的生成又过于抽象。因此，我们提出了AdaTutoRank，一种在三层级九个评分维度下通过自适应辅导优化（ATO）训练的集合式重排序器，为冷启动提供银标，为强化学习提供奖励，并为蒸馏提供提示。ATO从策略自身冻结快照中提取三种逐步增加特异性的提示形式：仅评分标准、在评分标准下由自选择器选择的兄弟集合、以及由自反思器对生成与兄弟集合的对比得出的反思；每次生成获得与其质量匹配的提示形式。在提示条件下的冻结教师和无提示快照下对该生成重新评分，将提示效果蒸馏为补充群体相对结果优势的词元级优势。在涵盖RAG、深度研究和集合式评估的十个基准测试中，AdaTutoRank在整体性能上达到最佳，同时发起的检索调用更少。

2. EmbodiedMemory-Bench: Benchmarking Embodied Memory for Long-Horizon Embodied Tasks

Abstract:Long-horizon embodied interaction requires agents to retain and continually update information about the environment as they observe, act, and encounter change. Yet current agents struggle to maintain such memory reliably. Our analysis traces this limitation to four key deficiencies: weak fine-grained visual memory, unreliable dynamic world-state tracking, failing to record world state revealed by interaction outcomes, and limited generalization from prior experience. However, existing benchmarks do not directly assess these memory capabilities during long-horizon embodied interaction. To address this gap, we introduce EmbodiedMemory-Bench (EMem-Bench), comprising 2,554 interactive episodes across four task families. EMem-Bench requires agents to build and update memory from interaction history, then use it to complete a later task by acting in the environment. We further present Embodied-Memorizer (EMem), an external memory system that organizes embodied experience into spatial, event, and scene memories. We also train EMem-8B, an 8B policy that manages and uses these memories. We evaluate a diverse range of open-source and proprietary MLLMs and representative multimodal memory systems. Results show that current models remain weak and uneven across the four challenges. Under matched backbones, EMem achieves the best overall performance among the evaluated memory systems and improves both open-source and proprietary models, while EMem-8B further improves over its backbone. Q: 这篇论文试图解决什么问题？ 这篇论文旨在解决\*\*具身智能体（embodied agents）在长期交互中记忆能力不足\*\*的问题，并填补现有基准测试在这一领域的评估空白。具体而言，论文围绕以下几个核心层面展开： ### 1. 识别具身记忆的关键缺陷 通过对先进多模态大语言模型（MLLM）在长时间交互任务中失败轨迹的深入分析，论文发现当前智能体的失败主要集中在四种具身记忆能力的缺失上： - \*\*细粒度视觉记忆薄弱\*\*：智能体难以记住在密集场景中偶然观察到的物体外观与位置； - \*\*动态世界状态跟踪不可靠\*\*：环境发生变化（如物体被移动）后，智能体仍依赖过时的观察而非最新状态； - \*\*未能记录交互结果揭示的世界状态\*\*：智能体忽略了失败交互（如抽屉锁着、抓取失败）所反馈的不可见状态信息； - \*\*有限的经验泛化能力\*\*：智能体无法从过去的纠正或成功模式中提炼规则，并将其迁移到新物体或新任务上。 ### 2. 填补现有基准测试的空白 论文指出，现有的具身智能体基准测试通常采用单轮问答形式，或要求智能体在无先验上下文的条件下直接探索环境并执行任务。这些设置\*\*无法直接评估\*\*智能体是否能够从长期的多模态交互历史中\*\*提取、巩固并复用\*\*记忆信息来指导后续行动。因此，需要一个专门评估“具身记忆”的交互式基准测试。 ### 3. 提出评估框架与记忆系统 为应对上述挑战，论文做出了两项核心贡献： - \*\*引入 EMem-Bench\*\*：一个包含 2,554 个可执行交互片段的记忆中心化基准测试，系统性地评估智能体在四类记忆任务（被动观察、动态跟踪、交互失败、经验泛化）中的表现。 - \*\*提出 Embodied-Memorizer (EMem)\*\*：一种外部记忆系统，将多模态交互经验组织为空间记忆、事件记忆与场景记忆，并训练策略模型（EMem-8B）来学习如何写入、检索和利用这些记忆以完成长期任务。 简言之，该论文试图解决的核心问题是：\*\*如何让具身智能体在长时间、多模态、动态变化的物理交互中，可靠地建立、更新和利用世界状态记忆，并提供相应的评估基准与方法论。\*\* Q2: 有哪些相关研究？ 该论文的相关研究可归纳为以下几个方向： ### 1. 具身智能体基准测试（Embodied-agent Benchmarks） 现有基准测试主要评估视觉驱动的智能体在不同动作层次上的能力，部分工作开始引入历史观测或状态轨迹： - \*\*EmbodiedBench\*\* (Yang et al., 2025a)： comprehensive benchmarking multi-modal large language models for vision-driven embodied agents. - \*\*FindingDory\*\* (Yadav et al., 2025) 与 \*\*LMEE-Bench\*\* (Wang et al., 2026a)：利用视觉历史进行导航任务。 - \*\*SpaMEM\*\* (Liao et al., 2026)：测试动态空间状态修正能力。 - \*\*WorldLines\*\* (Zhang et al., 2026)：评估跨越数天轨迹的状态问答与规划。 这些基准测试通常在单一任务设定中引入记忆需求，而 EMem-Bench 的差异化在于\*\*跨交互片段联合评估\*\*具身记忆能力。 ### 2. 智能体记忆评估（Agent Memory Evaluation） 针对 LLM 及多模态智能体的长期记忆能力，已有工作关注多会话历史中的信息保持与检索： - \*\*LoCoMo\*\* (Maharana et al., 2024) 与 \*\*LongMemEval\*\* (Wu et al., 2025)：评估智能体在极长期对话中的记忆保持。 - \*\*MemoryAgentBench\*\* (Hu et al., 2026)：研究检索能力与测试时学习（test-time learning）。 - \*\*Evo-Memory\*\* (Wei et al., 2026)：关注经验复用与自我进化记忆。 - \*\*WorldMemArena\*\* (Liu et al., 2026a)：系统评估多模态智能体记忆的写入、维护、检索与使用。 这些基准测试主要输出答案或重构知识，而 EMem-Bench 要求智能体\*\*将记忆转化为环境动作\*\*。 ### 3. 历史感知与长期具身交互基准 附录 A 进一步扩展了具有历史感知或长期范围的具身评估： - \*\*STARBench\*\* (Chen et al., 2025)：将时间回忆与开放世界物体检索中的空间动作相结合。 - \*\*RoboMME\*\* (Dai et al., 2026)：评估时间、空间、物体与程序记忆。 - \*\*RoboMemArena\*\* (Lei et al., 2026)：扩展至更长轨迹、组合子任务及真实世界成对任务。 - \*\*eMEM-Bench\*\* (Rasheed and Kabtoul, 2026)：围绕源监控、干扰与情境依赖检索展开。 - \*\*GOAT-Bench\*\* (Khanna et al., 2024)：研究终身导航中的场景表示。 - \*\*OST-Bench\*\* (Lin et al., 2025)：评估主动探索过程中的在线时空理解。 ### 4. 具身控制中的记忆架构 针对具身智能体的记忆系统设计，已有研究探索如何存储、检索和利用历史经验： - \*\*Memo\*\* (Gupta et al., 2025)：学习何时总结与检索历史以支持长期策略。 - \*\*MemoryVLA\*\* (Shi et al., 2026)：在工作记忆中结合感知细节与语义内容库。 - \*\*MEM\*\* (Torne et al., 2026)：用视频表示短期历史，用语言编码长期任务进度。 - \*\*STaR\*\* (Yuan et al., 2026)：构建任务无关的多模态记忆并检索任务条件子集。 - \*\*HIMM\*\* (Li et al., 2026)：分离情景回忆与可复用语义规则。 - \*\*MemCompiler\*\* (Ding et al., 2026)：基于当前执行状态进行条件化记忆选择，缓解静态检索与动态任务状态之间的错配。 - \*\*Affordance RAG\*\* (Korekata et al., 2025)：将检索到的视觉记忆 grounding 于可执行的移动操作选择。 ### 5. 从交互结果中学习 部分工作关注利用交互的成败反馈来更新世界状态或技能： - \*\*MEMO\*\* (Christie et al., 2026)：将局部的语言纠正与成功执行聚合为可重用的技能模板与操作指导。 ### 6. EMem-Bench 中对比评估的记忆系统 论文在实验部分还与以下代表性多模态记忆系统进行了对比： - \*\*MIRIX\*\* (Wang and Chen, 2025)：多智能体记忆系统。 - \*\*MemVerse\*\* (Liu et al., 2026b)：终身学习智能体的多模态记忆。 - \*\*TeleMem\*\* (Chen et al., 2026)：构建长期多模态记忆。 - \*\*MMA\*\* (Lu et al., 2026)：多模态记忆智能体。 Q3: 论文如何解决这个问题？ 论文通过\*\*构建专门的评估基准\*\*与\*\*设计结构化的外部记忆系统\*\*双管齐下地解决具身智能体长期记忆不足的问题。具体方案包含以下三个层面： ### 1. 构建记忆中心化的评估基准 EMem-Bench 为精准衡量具身记忆能力，论文设计了包含 \*\*2,554 个可执行交互片段\*\* 的基准测试，每个片段由以下要素构成： - 按时间排序的多模态交互历史 H = o_1^h, dots, o_N^h - 目标任务 T - 可行动作空间 A 智能体必须理解历史 H ，从中提取完成任务所需的信息，并在后续与环境的持续交互中逐步生成动作轨迹，直至达成目标：

at sim π(· mid H, T, o(1:t)^e, a_(1:t-1))
基准测试围绕四种互补的记忆缺陷，组织了四个任务家族： | 任务家族 | 核心评估能力 | 交互历史构造方式 | |—-|—-|—-| | **Passive Observation** | 细粒度视觉记忆 | 目标物体在密集场景中偶然出现，后续任务要求找回该物体 | | **Dynamic Tracking** | 动态世界状态跟踪 | 记录物体位置或状态的前后变化，后续任务要求基于**最新**状态行动 | | **Interaction Failure** | 记录交互结果揭示的状态 | 包含一次失败的物理交互及其反馈（如抽屉锁着），后续任务要求避开不可行选项 | | **Experience Generalization** | 经验泛化记忆 | 包含多个针对不同物体的纠正案例，后续任务要求将提炼出的规则迁移到新物体 | 此外，论文在构造过程中注入干扰轨迹（distractor trajectories），并通过四层自动验证（环境有效性、轨迹回放、证据泄漏检测、Oracle 回放）与人工审核，确保任务确实依赖历史记忆而非当前观察或先验知识。 ### 2. 提出结构化外部记忆系统 Embodied-Memorizer (EMem) EMem 是一个模块化的外部记忆系统，在交互过程中持续组织与更新世界状态，包含三种互补的记忆类型： - **空间记忆（Spatial Memory）** 维护一个场景限定的实体图（entity graph），跟踪物体的位置、状态及相互关系（如 `in`、`on`、`held_by`）。当获得新观测或反馈时，系统会**替换**冲突家族中的旧关系，确保图结构始终反映各物体的**最新已知状态**。例如，一本书从床上被移到桌上后，空间记忆会更新其位置关系，避免依赖过时状态。 - **事件记忆（Event Memory）** 按时间顺序追加保存动作、环境反馈及人工纠正。当多个相关纠正表达同一规律时，事件记忆会生成紧凑的聚合规则（aggregate rule），供后续检索。例如，尝试打开顶层抽屉失败后得知其被锁住，该结果会被保留，避免智能体在后续任务中重复尝试。 - **场景记忆（Scene Memory）** 将物体与交互绑定到具体场景（如厨房、卧室），防止不同房间中同名实体（如多个杯子）之间的混淆。它保存带有视觉上下文的快照、模型生成的描述及识别出的实体列表。 EMem 的运行遵循 **写入–检索–执行（write–retrieve–act）** 循环： 1. **历史写入阶段**：按时间顺序处理交互历史，调用 `update_object`、`remember_event`、`remember_scene` 将信息写入对应记忆； 2. **任务检索阶段**：面对目标任务时，智能体分解指令信息需求，选择查询一种或多种记忆（`query_spatial`、`query_event`、`query_scene`）； 3. **动作执行阶段**：将检索结果与当前观测、合法动作列表结合，生成并执行有序的动作序列；执行后的新观测与反馈再次触发记忆更新。 ### 3. 训练专用记忆策略 EMem-8B 为使智能体学会如何有效操作上述记忆，论文基于 **Qwen3-VL-8B** 进行监督微调（SFT），训练其掌握三项核心决策： - **写什么**：从当前观测与反馈中提取持久信息写入记忆； - **检索什么**：根据目标任务选择最相关的记忆查询； - **如何行动**：基于检索记忆与当前状态生成完整的动作序列。 训练数据通过在 **ProcTHOR** 训练集上由教师模型生成轨迹构建，包含 8,852 个监督回合（上下文写入、记忆查询、动作生成）。训练采用 LoRA（秩为 4），经过 1,816 步优化得到 **EMem-8B**。 ### 4. 实验验证与结论 论文系统评估了 16 个领先的开源与专有 MLLM。结果显示： - 当前最强模型（Gemini-3-Flash）的平均成功率（SR）也仅为 64.2%，开源模型大多低于 45%； - 在匹配骨干网络条件下，EMem 显著优于其他多模态记忆系统，在 Mistral-Small-3.1-24B 上平均 SR 提升 **16.4 点**，在 GPT-5.4-mini 上提升 **23.6 点**； - EMem-8B 相比其骨干网络 Qwen3-VL-8B 进一步提升 **20.6 点**； - 3. Program-Verified Self-Evolution for Vision-Language Models

Abstract:Self-evolving vision-language models train on questions they generate from unlabeled images. Since these questions have no gold answers, prior methods label them by majority vote over sampled answers or by a model judge. In a human evaluation, we find that 24\% of majority-vote labels and 18\% of model-judge labels produced during self-evolution are wrong. To address this problem, we present Verifiable QA Generation for Self-Evolving Models (VQS), which changes how the model judges answers. Instead of voting on an answer, the model parses each image into a structured record, such as a scene graph, a chart table, or a diagram graph. Fixed programs then write a question from the record and compute its answer. The model still acts as a visual checker, but it only confirms the individual facts the program reads, one short claim at a time. These claim-level checks select the parser’s training targets, so the parser also improves without labels. Human raters find 94\% of VQS answers correct, against 76\% for majority voting. Across ten benchmarks, VQS improves Qwen3-VL by up to 3.18 points at the 2B, 4B, and 8B scales and outperforms the strongest self-evolving baseline at each. Gains keep growing over three training rounds, reaching 3.84 points at 2B. Code is released at this https URL

4. YuE2: Unifying Symbolic and Audio Music Generation at Frontier Quality

Abstract:Symbolic models make melody, harmony, rhythm, and form explicit but typically stop before a finished recording; audio models produce complete songs while leaving composition implicit. We introduce YuE2, which unifies symbolic and audio music generation at frontier quality through symbolic planning. A single AR-NAR Mixture-of-Transformers (MoT) first writes a readable score specifying melody and harmony, expands it into semantic music tokens, and realizes it as full-song audio. In comparisons using the same checkpoint, experts prefer symbolic planning for overall quality and musicality, with 49.3% of overall preferences versus 34.6% without planning. Experts also favor the unified model over a separate language model and diffusion Transformer. On WildSongBench, YuE2 scores 6.73 on SongBench Global Avg, exceeding all evaluated public baselines. Selecting from eight candidates (best-of-8), YuE2 reaches 6.96, the highest observed mean among all evaluated systems. Expert listening further establishes its competitiveness with proprietary song generators, favoring best-of-8 over Suno v4.5 and yielding nearly balanced preferences against Suno v5. To learn this generation process from recordings without aligned scores, we introduce MERT2 and SheetSage2 to supply semantic and symbolic supervision. MERT2 sets a new state of the art in music representation learning, surpassing previous best results on 14 of 15 MARBLE metrics; SheetSage2 leads 12 of 15 benchmark-metric pairs in our lead-sheet transcription comparison. The same checkpoint follows score edits while largely preserving unedited musical content and generates zero-shot covers without cover-specific training. Its readable score also enables agentic music editing, with external language models translating user feedback into revisions of the composition.

5. Rethinking Training-Inference Mismatch in LLM Reinforcement Learning: Where It Arises and How to Correct It

Abstract:We study training-inference mismatch in reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards (RLVR) for large language models, where rollouts are sampled by an inference engine while gradients are computed by a training engine, and the two engines assign different probabilities to the same tokens. To account for this discrepancy in policy updates, we introduce calibrated importance sampling (CIS). CIS is motivated by an empirically supported logit-displacement characterization that expresses the mismatch as an additive displacement $\varepsilon_t$ in log-odds, determined by the per-logit perturbation before the softmax, whose distribution is approximately invariant to token confidence. This characterization motivates a confidence-aware truncation: large positive displacements are truncated at a single constant threshold, which maps back to an importance-ratio cap that tightens as token confidence increases. Theoretically, we show that CIS replaces the unbounded second moment that governs the error of exact importance sampling with a term bounded by a constant, at the cost of a bias controlled by the truncated excess. In evaluation across three mixture-of-experts models and five mathematical reasoning benchmarks, CIS achieves the highest five-benchmark average on all three models among the evaluated baselines. Diagnostic analyses show that CIS places less truncation bias on low-confidence tokens than truncated importance sampling, while upward clipping of small importance weights reduces held-out accuracy.

6. CompoWorld: Compositional Environment Scaling for General Agents

Abstract:Automatically generated environments provide a scalable source of interaction data for training general agents. However, existing approaches mainly generate tasks within a single environment, while real-world workflows require agents to connect information and actions across multiple services. We introduce Compositional Environment Scaling (\textbf{CompoWorld}), which expands the task space by composing a finite library of reusable services. Coding agents turn tool specifications into verified services with typed states and shared interfaces, while a world model handles tools that cannot be reliably implemented. A random-walk procedure connects services through dependency graphs, enabling the generation and verification of tasks that require information to flow across services. Verified trajectories support supervised fine-tuning (SFT), while our Completion-Focused Rubric Reward guides reinforcement learning (RL) toward full task completion by emphasizing criteria with lower pass rates within each rollout group. We construct 448 services exposing 10,130 tools and use 3K SFT trajectories and 1K RL tasks to train Qwen3.6-35B-A3B. Experimental results show that CompoWorld improves on its backbone by 9.17 points on average across eight benchmarks. On AutomationBench, it surpasses frontier models such as Claude Opus 4.6 and leads all compared agent-specialized 35B-A3B models.

7. Diffusion Reward Models

Abstract:Reward models underpin the alignment of large language models, yet the dominant designs reduce each prompt—response pair to a point estimate or to a distribution from a fixed parametric family. This is at odds with human preference, which is inherently multimodal: the same response can be reasonably judged in many ways, and no single family covers all of them. To better fit this structure, we introduce DRM, a Diffusion Reward Model that recasts reward modeling as conditional density estimation over $p(\mathbf{r}\mid x,y)$. Conditioned on a frozen LLM encoder, a lightweight Diffusion Transformer denoises Gaussian noise into a reward vector, placing no parametric assumption on the output distribution and naturally representing its multimodal structure. A single architecture handles both multi-attribute regression and pairwise preference data, and at inference $N$ samples form an empirical reward distribution that can be aggregated into a scalar, a variance, or quantiles. Across five benchmarks, DRM matches or surpasses baselines under matched data and backbone, stays competitive with much larger discriminative, distributional, and generative RMs despite its modest training scale, and recovers multimodal reward structure where conventional heads collapse to a point. Uncertainty-aware rejection and lower-confidence-bound (LCB) aggregation further demonstrate that DRM can exploit distributional information beyond a scalar reward to improve reward-model decisions. Downstream RLHF experiments additionally show that using DRM as the training-time reward leads to improved policy performance, directly validating the practical benefit of diffusion-based reward modeling for RLHF training.

8. ControlScope: Workflow Revision and Reliability in LLM Agents

Abstract:How much of a running workflow should a language model agent revise? ControlScope compares continuing generated code, editing the next tool call’s data arguments, and replacing the unfinished workflow from the same public execution state. The nested permissions separate available repairs from the actions an agent selects. We evaluate one-time and repeated reviews across filesystem tasks, ALFWorld, and AppWorld. Across two source programs per task and three reasoning-reviewer draws on 20 filesystem tasks, FULL completes 15-16 tasks versus 13 for KEEP; across four fast draws it completes 10-13 versus 13. Fresh student-record confirmation reproduces a batch-read repair. ALFWorld fast panels yield KEEP/ARG/FULL scores of 85/86/87 on 87 tasks across 52 scenes and 134/134/127 on 134 tasks across four scenes; reasoning on the 87-task cohort also yields 85/86/87 with substantial review cost. An AppWorld V1 official-test panel of 585 task instances from 195 scenario templates shows small net differences. Frozen replays expose viable agent-written replacements interrupted by later revision in two failed file-organization runs. An offline source-trajectory midpoint comparison shows later reviews completing an insufficient repair. Five-call protection saves 19.4% of logged model output and loses one success across 20 fresh source runs. An argument-only shortcut shows that the broader sampled policy can overlook a cheaper successful edit available in both operation sets. These outcomes tie repair access to actual choices and subsequent execution.

9. Fewer Tokens, More Self-Teaching: On-Policy Self-Distillation for Extreme Visual Token Reduction

Abstract:Visual token reduction is an effective way to accelerate multimodal large language models (MLLMs), but performance deteriorates rapidly under extremely low token budgets. Existing work has explored both visual-token selection and training-based adaptation to reduced visual inputs. We take a step further by asking how a heavily compressed MLLM should learn from the states induced by its own generations. This setting naturally calls for on-policy self-distillation: a heavily compressed model is supervised on the states induced by its own generations, while its full-token counterpart serves as an information-rich teacher. Based on this insight, we propose LT-OPD, a training framework for extreme visual-token reduction. The student rolls out responses with only a small fraction of visual tokens, and a frozen full-token copy of the same MLLM provides distributional supervision along these student-generated trajectories. To stabilize on-policy learning when visual evidence is severely limited, we further introduce a budget-level curriculum that progressively decreases the token budget during training. Across nine benchmarks on Qwen3.5-4B, LT-OPD raises average retained performance under 5% visual-token retention from 68.6% to 82.3%, outperforming training-free, training-based, and reinforcement-learning baselines at the same budget. The gains transfer consistently to Qwen3.5-9B, GLM-4.6V-9B, and LLaVA-OV-1.5-4B. LT-OPD also reduces KV-cache usage by 85.2% and prefill FLOPs by 85.4% without additional inference overhead, demonstrating that on-policy learning can substantially recover capabilities lost to extreme visual-token reduction.

摘要：视觉令牌（visual token）减少是加速多模态大型语言模型（MLLM）的一种有效方式，但在极低令牌预算下性能会迅速下降。现有工作已经探索了视觉令牌选择以及基于训练的方法来适应减少的视觉输入。我们进一步提出了一个问题：在强压缩的 MLLM 中，模型应如何从自身生成的状态中学习。在这种设置下，自然需要采用策略内自蒸馏（on-policy self-distillation）：一个高度压缩的模型在其自身生成状态上接受监督，而其完整令牌版本则作为信息丰富的教师。基于这一洞察，我们提出了 LT-OPD，一种用于极端视觉令牌减少的训练框架。学生模型仅使用少量视觉令牌生成响应，同时冻结的完整令牌 MLLM 副本沿着学生生成的轨迹提供分布监督。为了在视觉证据极度有限时稳定策略内学习，我们进一步引入了一个预算级课程，在训练过程中逐步减少令牌预算。在 Qwen3.5-4B 的九个基准测试中，LT-OPD 将 5% 视觉令牌保留下的平均保留性能从 68.6% 提升至 82.3%，在相同预算下优于无训练、基于训练和强化学习基线。该提升可以稳定迁移至 Qwen3.5-9B、GLM-4.6V-9B 和 LLaVA-OV-1.5-4B。LT-OPD 还减少了 85.2% 的 KV-cache 使用和 85.4% 的预填充 FLOPs，而不增加额外的推理开销，证明策略内学习能够显著恢复在极端视觉令牌减少中丢失的能力。
