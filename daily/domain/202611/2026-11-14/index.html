1. Abstention Is Not Threshold Compliance: Stated Scoring Rules in Open Language Models

Abstract:Kalai et al. propose stating the scoring rule in the prompt with an explicit threshold t, so that a model should answer only when its probability of being correct exceeds t. We probe each requirement of compliance on shared items, in 12 checkpoints from three post-training stages of four fully open 3-8B recipes, with exploratory models up to 32B. At t = 0.75, a line limiting the reply to an answer or “I abstain” (the abstention format) raised the mean abstention rate from 20% to 91%. Yet within this format, raising t from 0.5 to 0.9 changed it by only -0.8 percentage points. Without the line, the higher threshold even lowered abstention, and up to 40% of a checkpoint’s items received decisions that no compliant policy with threshold-independent beliefs could make. Given the probability that their answer was correct, the checkpoints barely responded to the threshold in a forced-choice probe. Comparisons with self-assessment signals such as P(True) depended on the grader and on answer drift between prompts. An external threshold on recalibrated P(True) made coverage non-increasing in t by construction. Calibrated on the evaluation distribution (post hoc), it beat the abstention format at t = 0.5; calibrated on TriviaQA and applied to PopQA, however, it fell below that format at 0.75. On OLMo 3 7B, decisions trained on threshold-conditioned targets did not follow unseen thresholds consistently. A post-hoc variant trained at four thresholds answered less as t rose but still failed a pre-specified test, mainly on agreement with its training rule. A high abstention rate is not evidence of compliance: evaluations using stated scoring rules should report how often models abstain, whether decisions respond to the stated threshold, and which items they answer.

2. MITRA-Swarm: Injecting Explicit Linguistic Structure into Foundation Models for Classical Machine Translation

Abstract:Classical Sanskrit poses three challenges unresolved by single-call monolithic models: morphophonemic fusion Sandhi, dense nominal compounding Samāsa, and discourse-level zero-anaphora forced by poetic meter. We present MITRA-Swarm, a multi-agent architecture injecting explicit linguistic structure into an otherwise monolithic pipeline: a Sandhi-splitting agent, a parallel array of generalist gemma-4-26B-A4B-it and domain-specialized gemma-2-mitra-it translators, and a discourse-aware Ellipsis Restorer that consults corpus-derived register tags to avoid inventing active-voice subjects in impersonal legal and philosophical registers. Two separated judges evaluate quality: a routing judge ($J{\text{route}}$, same model family as the generators) driving an internal critique loop, and an independent reporting judge ($J{\text{report}}=$gpt-4o-mini, no shared vendor lineage) scoring every output exactly once. On a stratified benchmark of 1,000 verses across five registers (23,990 scored verdicts, 12-condition pre-registered ablation), the full swarm reaches $85.90$ MQM against a monolithic baseline of $85.27$ ($\Delta=+0.63$, $p{\text{adj}}=0.0018$, 95% CI $[+0.30,+0.97]$), and isolated discourse restoration alone accounts for $+0.44$ MQM ($p{\text{adj}}=0.0288$), outperforming a call-count-matched self-refinement baseline by $+0.51$ MQM ($p{\text{adj}}=0.0288$). Severe per-verse failures (MQM$<80$) fall from $9.0\%$ to $7.6\%$, at roughly $9\times$ the per-verse inference cost of the baseline. Additionally, $J{\text{route}}$ scores its own family’s outputs $2$-$7$ MQM higher than $J_{\text{report}}$ on every one of the 12 conditions, empirically motivating the two-judge split; and a preflight screen of a 192,151-entry retrieval index against all 12 evaluation works verifies 0 reference leaks across 269 runtime checks. These are small gains on a metric saturating near 85-86.

3. Probe What-If Worlds Before You Ship: Persona Simulation for Real-World Decision Making

Abstract:User research and system testing reveal whether products and services meet user needs, yet recruiting specialized participants, designing controlled what-if comparisons, and repeating studies as designs evolve often bottleneck design iteration and data-driven decision-making. We present a framework and execution infrastructure that translates real-world industry inquiries into controlled experiments with simulated users. Our methodology formally defines the target cohort, experimental stimuli, and decision-relevant metrics, while the pipeline supports large-scale concurrent runs over cohort-stimulus-environment combinations (survey, web, chatbot, and OS app), auditable trial-level logging, cohort-level outcome aggregation, and cross-cohort and cross-variant statistical reports. Across twelve industry partner cases spanning five domains, we show how constructing hard-to-reach or what-if persona cohorts supports exploratory studies and distills simulated responses into testable design hypotheses. Cross-model comparisons on selected cases largely preserve primary conclusions, whereas perturbing or ablating persona attributes alters service outcomes, effect sizes, and inter-group differences, indicating that grounding target cohorts in detailed personas materially shapes simulation results. Together, these studies show that persona-enabled agents can test specified cohorts at design speed, diagnose systems under test, and deliver directional evidence that focuses problem definition, steers design and strategic decisions, and guides further study.

4. Benchmarking Multilingual Document Understanding in Vision-Language Models

Abstract:Recent Vision-Language Models (VLMs) perform strongly on multimodal tasks, Document Understanding (DU) among them, but the benchmarks that measure them mostly cover English documents and narrow problems. How well current VLMs generalize across languages, tasks, styles and layouts therefore remains unclear. We introduce MDU, a multilingual DU benchmark of 23146 examples spanning 13 tasks and five languages: English, French, German, Italian, and Spanish. The tasks isolate complementary capabilities, from surface-level text and Markdown extraction through summarization, classification and named-entity recognition to layout-sensitive passage and layout extraction. We evaluate 45 state-of-the-art open-weight VLMs from 1.7B to 235B parameters, plus one commercial system under the same protocol. The benchmark is far from saturated: the best average is 69.0, only eight of the 45 evaluated systems reach 65, and the two best results come from 27B models rather than from the largest systems we evaluate. Performance is uneven in ways that scale does not fix. The gap between the strongest and weakest language does not close as models grow, and it survives controlling for the domain composition of each language. Capabilities also do not improve together: once we partial out overall model quality, per-task scores are largely uncorrelated, and transcription ability in particular trades off against semantic extraction.

摘要：近期的视觉-语言模型（VLMs）在多模态任务中表现出色，其中包括文档理解（DU），但用于衡量它们的基准大多覆盖英文文档和较窄的问题。因此，当前VLMs在语言、任务、风格和布局上的泛化能力仍不明确。我们提出了MDU，这是一个多语言文档理解基准，包含23146个样本，覆盖13个任务和五种语言：英文、法文、德文、意大利文和西班牙文。这些任务分隔了互补的能力，从表层文本和Markdown提取，到摘要、分类和命名实体识别，再到对布局敏感的段落和布局提取。我们在同一协议下评估了45个最先进的开源权重VLMs，参数规模从1.7B到235B，以及一个商业系统。该基准远未饱和：最佳平均分为69.0，45个评估系统中仅有八个达到65，且两个最佳结果来自27B模型，而不是我们评估的最大系统。性能存在不均衡现象，而规模并不能解决这一问题。最强和最弱语言之间的差距随着模型增长并未缩小，即使控制了每种语言的领域构成，这种差距仍然存在。能力也不会同步提升：一旦我们剔除整体模型质量，每个任务的得分基本不相关，尤其是在转录能力与语义提取能力之间存在权衡。

5. An LLM Is All You Need: Data-Efficient Adaptation to Full-Duplex, Word-Level Spoken Dialogue

Abstract:Full-duplex spoken dialogue, in which a system listens while it speaks and can backchannel, yield the floor, and be interrupted, is usually obtained by training on hundreds of thousands to millions of hours of speech. This is costly, rewrites the language model that supplies the system’s knowledge, and still leaves turn-taking either implicit or decided coarsely rather than word by word. We show that a frozen, general-purpose multimodal LLM can instead be adapted into a word-level full-duplex system with little data. Our system is a thinker-talker cascade on an 80 ms frame clock. The thinker keeps the LLM frozen and trains only lightweight adapters and a few new token embeddings (97M parameters); at every frame it emits one token of agent text, with pauses, yields, and onsets represented as tokens of the same vocabulary and kept legal by a finite-state machine. A four-stage curriculum, each stage anchored to the model’s own behavior under the previous format, performs the conversion in about 30 GPU-hours. The talker reads only the thinker’s discrete tokens, so the two halves are trained independently. On Full-Duplex-Bench and InteractionBench, the system is competitive with open-source full-duplex systems built through large-scale speech training: among them, it is the least likely to take the floor when the user merely pauses, backchannels most often, and leads in spoken-task accuracy, groundedness, and coherence over long conversations.

p_θ(y_t mid h_t, s_t) = softmax(z_t + mask(s_t)), quad mask(s_t)_v = 0, & v ∈ legal(s_t) -∞, & otherwise
状态转移 δ 定义了三种核心状态： - **SILENCE**（监听态）：合法 token 仅为 PAD 或 SE。若采样 SE，自动在下一帧前插入 EPAD，随后进入 CONTENT。 - **CONTENT**（说话态）：可采样词片、pause _0 （继续）、pause _m （进入 PAUSE 队列）或 SS（让权进入 SILENCE）。 - **PAUSE**（停顿态）：不采样，而是从 FIFO 队列中自动弹出 PAD/EPAD，直至队列清空后返回 CONTENT。 该设计将 turn-taking 分解为每帧的合法动作选择，既实现了**逐词级粒度**，又保证了生成序列的语法合法性。 —- ### 3. Thinker–Talker 离散接口（解决耦合问题） 系统采用级联架构，但两个模块之间**仅传递离散 token ID**，不传递任何连续隐状态： - **Thinker**：冻结 LLM + 可训练适配器，输出离散的智能体文本流（含控制 token）。 - **Talker**：基于 Moshi 架构的流式语音合成器，独立训练。它使用自身的 token 嵌入表接收 thinker 的输出，并自回归地预测 8 层残差 codec token，经 Mimi 解码器还原为波形。 由于接口完全是离散的，Talker 可直接在**文本-语音对齐数据**上进行监督训练（预测 8 个 codebook 的交叉熵），无需 Thinker 参与数据生成或联合优化。这使得： - 两个模块**训练顺序任意**，可独立迭代； - Thinker 的改动不影响 Talker 的训练数据 pipeline； - 实时推理时，文本流领先音频流 3 帧（240 ms），为 Talker 提供必要的 lookahead 以保证合成流畅度。 —- ### 4. 实时约束与增量计算 全双工要求系统在同一时间线上持续听与说。论文将实时性作为**设计约束**而非仅事后测量： - 每 80 ms 的音频输入必须在 80 ms 墙钟时间内完成 Thinker 与 Talker 的前向传播； - Audio adapter 仅编码**新到达的当前帧**，计算成本不随对话长度增长； - 全程使用 KV-cache 增量解码，避免对前缀的二次重计算； - 即使在静默期，系统也保持前向传播，从而随时响应用户的打断（barge-in）。 Q4: 论文做了哪些实验？ 论文的实验围绕**公开基准评测**与**内部机制分析**两部分展开，具体包括以下内容： —- ## 1. 基准测试与评估协议 ### 1.1 评测基准 论文在三个外部基准上进行了全双工行为与对话质量的端到端评估： - **Full-Duplex-Bench v1.0**

Thinker 话轮转换模型的复杂化 当前有限状态机仅有 **SILENCE / CONTENT / PAUSE** 三个状态，且状态转移为硬编码： - 可引入更细粒度的会话状态（如 **HESITATION**、**OVERLAP-NEGOTIATION**、**PREDICTIVE-TAKE**），以支持更自然的协商式重叠（negotiated overlap）与预测性接话； - 探索**可学习的状态合法性掩码**，例如通过约束优化或强化学习让模型在保持序列合法性的同时，自主发现更优的状态转移策略，而非依赖人工设计的 FSM； - 将框架从双人对话扩展到**多人会议场景**，此时话轮转换涉及更复杂的听者选择与打断目标判定。 —- ### 3. Talker 的感知增强与生成控制 论文的 Talker 当前将用户音频通道置零，且在 InteractionBench 的**耳语与音量控制**维度表现较弱： - **启用用户音频通道**：利用 Talker 架构中已有的 Uk 嵌入，让合成语音在音色、语速、情感上自适应地匹配用户特征（声学共鸣），或实现零样本语音克隆； - **韵律与响度控制**：在训练目标中引入响度 token 或连续韵律条件，使系统能执行耳语、强调、情绪表达等指令； - 6. Distributionally Robust BEST-RQ For Speech Representation Learning

Abstract:BEST-RQ, based on random projection and quantization, has been a well-established self-supervised learning approach to learn speech representations from large-scale unlabeled audio due to its high performance and efficiency. 8. Robust Task-Aware State Estimation from Pre-trained Perception

Abstract:Agents that perform state estimation using pretrained perception models, e.g., Vision-Language Models (VLMs), face the fundamental challenge that predictions are uncertain. Still, most approaches determinize these predictions into a single best-guess state and make decisions as if it were ground truth. This is brittle because when the guess is wrong, the agent’s decisions may be unsafe or infeasible. At the other extreme, maintaining a full probability distribution over symbolic states is often intractable. We propose a middle path, drawing on the underlying probabilities the perception model already produces (e.g., a VLM’s output-layer token distributions) to construct a $\theta$-confidence Minimal Belief Set (MBS$\theta$), a minimal subset of states whose cumulative probability meets a user-specified belief-mass threshold. Using a novel three-layered belief system, we efficiently approximate individual state probabilities from per-feature predictions and task-level constraints. On this belief construction, we base our MBS$\theta$ construction, while maintaining only a lean factored representation of the belief. Our algorithm, Probability-Ranked Valid State Search (PRaVaSS), takes advantage of the unique structure of this belief system to construct an MBS$\theta$ with correctness guarantees and polynomial complexity in the unconstrained case. Using a VLM as the backbone of our state estimator, we validate our method’s theoretical properties, scalability with problem size, and improvement of downstream decision-making when used in a task-planning pipeline.

9. Sophon: A Procedural Diagnostic for Spatial Reasoning in Vision-Language Models

Abstract:Open-weight Vision-Language Models (VLMs) score 85-94% on spatial benchmarks (BLINK Spatial, What’s Up CLEVR) yet collapse to chance on simple geometric questions when object, scene, and texture priors are stripped away. We introduce and open-source Sophon, a procedurally generated diagnostic of paired colored point-cloud stimuli across three coordinate systems (1D linear, 2D Cartesian, 2D polar) and four question types (binary, categorical, comparative, quantitative). Across four open architectures (LLaVA-NeXT, LLaVA-OneVision, Qwen2.5-VL, Idefics3), accuracy clusters at 47-52% while frontier models (Opus 4.6, Gemini 2.5 Pro, GPT-5.4) clear 77-83% and humans reach 84-94%. 10. Paired Geometric Supervision for Generalizable Deepfake Detection

Abstract:We present OrthoPairGTL, a geometric training framework for cross-domain deepfake detection, built on a partially frozen DINOv3 backbone ($\sim$300M parameters), and make three contributions: (1) We re-evaluate six recent transfer-learning detectors (Forensics Adapter, Effort, three GenD variants and FakeSTormer) using the authors’ released checkpoints under a single preprocessing pipeline across 17 cross-domain datasets (2018-2025), so that the reported gaps reflect method rather than per-method pipeline tuning. (2) We model the transformation between paired real and fake samples, training Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning (PEFT) adapters with an orthogonal pair decomposition, hyperspherical regularisation, and (in the GSD-LoRA variant) a set-level coherence objective. (3) Under LoRA, this regularisation gives a more stable optimisation profile: GSD-LoRA’s post-peak validation degradation is $4.2\times$ shallower than plain LoRA at the same parameter footprint, and the time-averaged validation AUC across training is 1.82 pp higher. On the 17 datasets, GSD-LoRA matches or exceeds every re-evaluated baseline (92.82% vs. 91.58% for the strongest; paired Wilcoxon signed-rank $p=0.015$, 13/17 per-dataset wins), with the largest gains on modern reenactment-heavy benchmarks (DSv1.1, DSv2).
