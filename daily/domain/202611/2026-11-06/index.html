1. Discourse Analysis of Legislative Bill Discussions

Abstract:Legislative hearings are distinct communication events with their own human recognizable structure and logic, yet they have been analyzed as distinct phenomena beyond their transcripts. Understanding legislative hearings at a deeper level is a major step in analyzing the work and power relationships within democratic bodies. In this work, we present a novel end-to-end pipeline for parsing rhetorical structures from legislative committee hearings with the goal of improving legislative discourse analysis and democratic accountability. We also propose a rhetorical structure grammar and find that 55.21% of California state bill discussions in our dataset can be parsed with this grammar. We evaluate our pipeline with two utterance section classifiers on a random subset of over 2,000 annotated utterances corresponding to 100 hearings. We find that a logistic regression-based classifier with grammar-based output masking and utterance tag smoothing outperforms more complex models, achieving an F1 score of 52.8% against our ground truth discourse section labels.

3. CCDWIKI: Decoupling Case Formulation from Response Generation in Longitudinal CBT Dialogue

Abstract:Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) demands more than empathic validation, requiring instead a structured formulation of the interplay among situations, automatic thoughts, emotions, and behaviors. Therapists formalize this interplay through a Cognitive Conceptualization Diagram, where maintaining longitudinal coherence necessitates continuous evidential adjudication to separate tentative hypotheses from established findings. Current dialogue systems track conversational history via retrieval or static profiles but lack the representational capacity to encode epistemic status. Consequently, provisional interpretations are often prematurely reified as established facts, inadvertently biasing downstream interventions. Addressing this limitation, CCDWiki is introduced as a plug-and-play advisor conceptualizing longitudinal therapy as evidence-grounded hypothesis adjudication. The approach encodes each clinical hypothesis as a mutable entry bound to corroborating and contradicting evidence. Across sessions, accumulating data strengthens supported entries, preserves provisional ones, and retracts contradicted assumptions. The resulting formulation distills into concise guidance for unmodified therapeutic language models. Experiments demonstrate substantial improvements in both conceptualization and intervention quality without requiring backbone retraining.

4. Human-like cross-language generalisation in deep neural speaker embeddings and its acoustic foundations

Abstract:Generalisability is a central question for deep neural networks, including the cross-language generalisation of speaker embeddings. This study examined whether deep neural speaker embeddings generalise across languages and how such generalisation relates to human perception and acoustic structure. Using Cantonese—English bilingual speech presented in within- and cross-language conditions, we derived similarity from 18 state-of-the-art models and compared it with human ratings and acoustic features using linear mixed models and representational similarity analysis. Embeddings exhibited cross-language generalisation that broadly paralleled human behaviour, reflecting accurate identity discrimination and sensitivity to language switching. This generalisation relied on largely shared acoustic cues with language-sensitive weighting, mirroring human patterns. Together, these findings provide converging evidence for human—machine alignment in cross-language voice similarity processing.

5. Same Origin, Different Verdicts: Text Production Conditions Confound Machine-Generated Text Detection in Russian

Abstract:Automated detection of machine-generated text is applied where the verdict carries a cost for the author, yet origin is a property of the production process, not an observable property of the text. The conditions under which a text is produced push it in the same direction in which a generative model is presumed to push it. This study measures the contribution of these conditions while origin is held fixed. The Russian-language corpus of 1882 documents comprises a machine part, generated by four generation channels (each backed by its own model family) across 45 tasks in three task modes, and an archival human part annotated for source regimentation level before the texts were read. The primary contrast is computed within the machine corpus: both halves of a pair are generated by the same model for the same task and differ only in task wording. Machine-likeness is measured by four operationalisations: an interpretable index with weights fixed before data collection, a trained classifier, a zero-shot metric on an open model, a judge model. The main result is disagreement between procedures: changing the task mode shifted the scores of all four, but three gave a negative shift, the judge a positive one, and none of the four intervals covers zero. The comparison plan and the specifications of procedures 2-4 were fixed after the result of procedure 1, before procedures 2-4 ran; the disagreement survived correction of a corpus defect and of the nested validation. A baseline on the source identifier alone reaches a median AUROC of 1.0000 on the thirteen group splits where it is defined, and removing emphasis markers flipped a confident machine verdict to human without changing the full text by a single character. The scores are properties of a specific measurement system and corpus: they reflect data collection, repetition, formatting, and preprocessing decisions as well as linguistic style.

6. SRRM: A Retrieval-Reconstruction Benchmark for Evaluating Long-Context Memory in Small Language Models

Abstract:Small Language Models (SLMs) are increasingly deployed on consumer-grade hardware, yet their ability to preserve information across progressively longer contexts remains insufficiently understood. Existing benchmarks primarily evaluate retrieval-oriented tasks, providing limited insight into whether successful retrieval reflects comprehensive long-context memory preservation. To address this limitation, this paper introduces Simple Retrieval-Reconstruction Memory (SRRM), a lightweight and fully reproducible benchmark that jointly evaluates Targeted Retrieval and Global Information Reconstruction. This study also proposes the Memory Preservation Ratio (MPR), a benchmark-level metric that quantifies the relationship between retrieval and reconstruction performance. SRRM was evaluated using a synthetic dataset on three Qwen2.5 models (0.5B, 1.5B, and 3B parameters) across context loads ranging from 10 to 100 factual statements. Experimental results show that reconstruction performance deteriorated substantially faster than retrieval accuracy as contextual information increased, indicating that retrieval alone is insufficient to characterize long-context memory preservation. Among the evaluated models, Qwen2.5-1.5B achieved the highest retrieval accuracy (86.7%), reconstruction performance (45.7%), and MPR (0.53), outperforming the larger Qwen2.5-3B model (72.2%, 18.5%, and 0.26, respectively). These findings demonstrate that increasing parameter count does not necessarily improve long-context memory preservation. Overall, SRRM provides a reproducible and extensible benchmark for systematically evaluating long-context memory while revealing complementary memory behaviors that remain hidden under retrieval-only evaluation frameworks.

8. When to Ask, When to Answer: Strategic Clarification for Ambiguous Reference Resolution in Dialogue

Abstract:For humans, referential disambiguation is a collaborative process involving clarification questions asked when they will meaningfully change the outcome. However, large language models (LLMs) have been shown to be biased toward premature answering, preferring definite single-turn responses over more clarification exchanges. In this paper, we study the problem of “strategic clarification” — asking clarification questions in a reference game when their answers will narrow the set of candidate referents. We construct a controlled test environment from the SIMMC 2.1 dataset that records how many questions each ambiguous reference needs to be successfully resolved, and examine how baseline LLM behavior and common post-training methods ask clarifying questions vs. attempt to answer the referent. We also propose BACE (Belief-Aware Critic with Epistemic tracking), a lightweight action chooser method on top of a frozen LM backbone, and show that BACE more consistently chooses the optimal number of clarifying questions than methods such as GRPO and ArCHer.

9. Rewrite, Then Veto: Rewriter-Agnostic Edit Gating for LLM-Based Post-OCR Correction

Abstract:LLMs can repair noisy OCR transcripts of historical documents, but they also change text that was already correct. We propose EditVeto, a veto gate placed after a full-context LLM rewrite. The rewrite is character-aligned to the OCR input, its differences are split into blocks, and a 0.6B-parameter gate keeps or reverts each block. An oracle analysis motivates the design: given the true error locations, per-span generation is worse than no correction, whereas vetoing a full rewrite’s edits helps. Trained on rewrites by two Qwen3 models and evaluated, frozen, on the eight HIPE-OCRepair-2026 test sets, the gate improves all five rewriters tested, two of them unseen in training: weighted cMER falls by 0.0026-0.0044 and the preference score rises by 0.15-0.25 (paired bootstrap, p ~ 0.0002 each). On published competition outputs it lowers cMER for three of four teams but raises it for the winning run (0.0050 to 0.0065). In a second, disclosed test look, a retrained gate still improves LoRA-tuned rewriters (cMER -0.0011 to -0.0021), and an ensemble of their gated edits reaches a cMER of 0.0069 and a preference score of 0.928, mostly through fine-tuning and pooling rather than gating. Built only on public models and data, it is behind the winner (0.0050) and tied with the second-ranked run (0.0071), both from the only team using domain pre-training, and ahead of all other published runs. A third, secondary look shows the unchanged gate improving zero-shot Qwen3.8-27B (cMER 0.0114 to 0.0108) but only the preference score of its LoRA-tuned version.

9. MA-SafeDiffuser: Safe Multi-Agent Planning with Diffusion Probabilistic Models

Abstract:We propose MA-SafeDiffuser, a multi-agent extension of SafeDiffuser that equips diffusion-based trajectory planners with finite-time diffusion invariance guarantees for joint-agent safety specifications. 10. Towards Efficient Whole Page Ranking with One-Pass Inference

Abstract:Whole-page ranking jointly optimizes advertisements and organic content to balance business outcomes and user engagement under complex layout and business constraints. Existing non-autoregressive (NAR) approaches enable efficient page generation but typically score candidate items independently of previously selected items, limiting their ability to capture order-dependent interactions. Autoregressive (AR) methods address this limitation through sequential conditioning, but incur substantial inference overhead that challenges large-scale deployment. Bridging this quality-efficiency gap remains a challenge in industrial whole-page ranking. We propose Multi-Item Scoring (MIS), a prefix-aware NAR-style framework that incorporates sequential context into efficient multi-item scoring. MIS integrates global candidate interactions, localized prefix conditioning, and position-aware scoring within a shared representation architecture. By computing reusable representations once per request and performing lightweight prefix-conditioned scoring during constrained beam search, MIS captures important sequential dependencies without repeated full-model inference. Experiments on KuaiRand-1K and RL4RS demonstrate that MIS approaches the generation quality of an autoregressive CGA baseline while achieving 4.07x and 3.54x faster CPU decoding, respectively. In a randomized evaluation on a large-scale social-video platform, MIS improves the realization metric by 0.104% and watch time by 0.058%, while reducing ranking-service CPU consumption by 9.73%. These results demonstrate that prefix-aware multi-item scoring can narrow the quality gap between NAR and AR generation while retaining the efficiency required for industrial scalability.

1. Who May Overrule the Agent? Evidence-Gated Authority in LLM Review Institutions

Abstract:Multi-agent language-model systems often add a reviewer to improve an upstream agent’s decision. Yet a reviewer can both repair errors and replace decisions that were already correct. 3. Uncertainty-Aware Resource Orchestration for Agentic AI Systems

Abstract:Agentic artificial intelligence (AI) systems powered by large language models (LLMs) execute as autonomous multi-step pipelines that dynamically invoke tools, integrate observations into memory, and iterate until a task is resolved or a resource budget is exhausted. This unpredictability forces infrastructure orchestrators into a dilemma: under-provisioning risks mid-execution throttling and deadline violations, while over-provisioning wastes scarce edge or cloud capacity. Existing schedulers require a fully known pipeline graph, while edge resource-management methods rely on parametric or stationary demand models, and neither fits the unknown, non-stationary profiles of agentic pipelines. We formalize this setting as the uncertainty-aware agentic orchestration problem, where the goal is to reserve, at dispatch time, compute and bandwidth allocations that cover actual pipeline demands, and to verify that the pipeline can meet its deadline, each with a prescribed probability. We then propose Conformal Reservation for Orchestration (CRO), an online algorithm that translates a sliding window of past execution traces into dispatch-time resource allocations with finite-sample, distribution-free coverage guarantees via conformal prediction. Simulations over heterogeneous task mixes with multi-modal and heavy-tailed profiles show that CRO is the only evaluated method that simultaneously satisfies target probabilistic coverage across all risk levels while, among coverage-feasible methods, attaining the lowest over-reservation, roughly halving the resource waste of the Chebyshev-bound baseline.

Abstract:Tiered inference distributes large language model computation across a hierarchy of devices, from on-device and edge hardware to the datacenter, and is increasingly offered as the route to cheaper, lower-latency, and more private serving. Q: 这篇论文试图解决什么问题？ 该论文旨在解决\*\*现有AI Agent基准测试与真实知识工作场景之间存在显著差距\*\*的问题。具体而言，当前评估体系难以捕捉协作式研究/工程环境中所需的复杂、多源、长程推理能力。 核心痛点可归纳为以下几个方面： - \*\*孤立化评估的局限\*\*：现有基准多依赖合成数据或单一任务，将网页浏览、软件工程、长程记忆等能力割裂测试，未能反映真实项目中多参与方动态、信息分散与决策演化的复杂性。 - \*\*多源信息联合推理的缺失\*\*：在真实工作场景中，项目状态分散于Slack对话、GitHub仓库、Google Docs及实验输出之中。回答一个简单问题往往需要定位相关频道、恢复长讨论上下文、检查代码状态、调和矛盾或过时的证据，并跨模态综合信息。现有基准极少要求智能体在保护隐私的前提下，基于真实代码仓库与协作对话进行联合推理。 - \*\*缺乏可复现的协作环境基准\*\*：虽已有针对工具使用、软件工程或多平台记忆的评测，但尚无可共享、可复现的基准能够同时评估智能体在异构工作场所应用（通信、文档、代码）与真实协作历史中的综合表现，特别是在研究/工程团队中部署助手时所需的对话导航与仓库交互能力。 为此，论文提出了 \*\*SlackBench\*\*，一个基于隐私保护的真实学术研究项目重现所构建的基准测试，通过评估智能体在异构来源搜索、幻觉回避、目标导向摘要及长对话情境推断等任务上的表现，系统性衡量其在真实协作工作流中的能力边界。 Q2: 有哪些相关研究？ 根据论文第2节，相关研究可归纳为以下三个主要方向： ### 1. 智能体与工具使用基准（Agent and tool-use benchmarks） 该方向侧重于评估LLM智能体在多环境中的动作选择与工具调用能力，但通常不涉及从长程、噪声化的多方工作历史中恢复潜在项目状态。 - \*\*AgentBench\*\*：建立多环境交互式评估框架。 - \*\*API-Bank\*\*：研究API规划与调用。 - \*\*ToolLLM\*\*：将工具使用数据规模扩展至数千个API。 - \*\*BFCL\*\*：标准化函数调用（function-calling）评估。 - \*\*ToolSandbox\*\*：引入有状态、对话式的工具执行评估。 - \*\*τ-bench\*\*：在领域特定策略下评估动态用户-智能体交互。 这些基准为衡量动作选择和工具正确性奠定了基础，但\*\*一般不要求智能体从分散在通信与代码中的长程、噪声化、多方工作历史中恢复潜在的项目状态\*\*。 ### 2. 多平台企业、记忆与个人上下文基准（Multi-platform enterprise, memory, and personal-context benchmarks） 该方向关注智能体在跨平台组织工作流或个人助手场景中的表现，与SlackBench最为接近但侧重点不同。 - \*\*AutomationBench\*\*：在相对较小的工作空间环境中评估智能体能力（中位环境仅3.3k token）。 - \*\*APEX-Agents\*\*：针对银行、咨询和法律等特定领域进行评估。 - \*\*Workspace-Bench\*\*：使用完全合成环境，并常直接指示智能体查看或忽略特定文件。 - \*\*MEMTRACK\*\*：跨工作场所工具交错事件，评估多平台组织工作流中的长期记忆与状态跟踪。 - \*\*Agent-Diff\*\*：通过代码执行与基于状态差异（state-diff）的成功标准评估企业API任务。 - \*\*ASTRA-bench\*\*：研究用户画像或时间演化的个人上下文在工具使用中的作用。 SlackBench与这些方向形成互补，\*\*专注于协作项目理解\*\*——答案不仅依赖记忆事实，更需要发现哪些对话关键、协调社会与技术证据、在前提错误时放弃回答、以及从多方行为中推断角色或倾向。 ### 3. 软件工程基准与Harness评估（Software-engineering benchmarks and harness evaluation） 该方向聚焦代码任务的智能体评估，并强调agent-computer interface（智能体-计算机接口）设计对性能的影响。 - \*\*SWE-bench\*\*：证明真实GitHub问题可为自主代码工作提供高难度测试平台。 - \*\*SWE-agent\*\*：证明智能体-计算机接口设计能显著提升性能。 - \*\*OpenHands\*\*：为构建和评估软件智能体提供灵活平台。 - \*\*MLE-bench\*\*：将评估扩展至机器学习工程领域。 - \*\*Terminal-Bench\*\*：针对受真实工作流启发的终端高难度任务。 - \*\*SkillsBench 与 AgencyBench\*\*：明确研究技能或脚手架（scaffolds）的影响，强调智能体性能不仅取决于基础模型质量。 SlackBench延续这一研究脉络进行配对的基础模型与harness评估，但所处的\*\*社会技术工作流（socio-technical workflow）要求成功同时依赖于对话导航与仓库交互\*\*。 ### 与现有工作的核心区别 现有基准分别研究了工具使用、软件工程、工作场所自动化和多平台记忆，但\*\*尚未直接评估智能体在隐私保护的真实协作项目历史重现上的表现——其中答案必须从多方Slack式通信与GitHub仓库状态中进行联合推理与整合\*\*。 Q3: 论文如何解决这个问题？ 论文通过构建并发布 \*\*SlackBench\*\* 这一基准测试与评估框架来解决该问题，具体方法涵盖环境构建、任务设计、系统评估与失败分析四个层面： ### 1. 构建隐私保护的真实协作环境 为避免暴露私人组织聊天记录，同时保留真实工作流的复杂性，论文采用\*\*基于真实学术研究项目的、人在回路中的隐私保护重现（privacy-preserving, human-in-the-loop simulations）\*\*方法： - \*\*对话生成\*\*：在真实Slack工作区中直接生成对话，再导出至本地目录。约35.0%的消息由人类直接撰写，35.2%由人类大幅改写，确保对话真实自然。 - \*\*角色扮演\*\*：人类扮演项目负责人，多个编码智能体（如Cursor、Claude Code）会话充当向其汇报的研究助理。 - \*\*多平台关联\*\*：每个Slack频道关联一个或多个GitHub仓库及Google Docs，模拟真实研究场景中的会议记录、IRB申请、代码开发与实验输出等。人类或智能体以自然方式在Slack中分享GitHub与Google Docs链接。 - \*\*精细控制\*\*：通过系统提示与上下文内提示结合，引导智能体的非编码行为（Slack消息风格、GitHub操作），同时保留真实研究组的多样化对话动态。 - \*\*后期清洗\*\*：导出后人工过滤改写过于AI化的消息、替换真实API密钥、纠正与仓库不符的幻觉内容。 最终生成包含15个频道（约3000条消息）、14个GitHub仓库、9个Google Docs的可复现环境，完整保留时间线、任务依赖与制品演化。 ### 2. 设计四类核心评估任务 论文构建140个任务，覆盖智能体在真实协作场景中必需的四种能力，这些任务无法简化为静态语料库问答： - \*\*Agentic Search (AS, 58项)\*\*：评估在异构、跨时间线数据中的深度信息检索能力。智能体需跨Slack频道、GitHub仓库、Google Docs甚至外部互联网搜索，并正确追踪项目与GitHub操作时间线。 - \*\*Hallucination (HA, 48项)\*\*：评估在不可回答或前提错误问题上的拒绝与弃权能力。包括答案未知、错误前提、上下文限制三种子场景，要求智能体不仅拒绝回答，还需正确识别拒绝原因。 - \*\*Summarization (SU, 14项)\*\*：评估面向特定目标的跨源信息汇总能力。例如为研究助理撰写推荐信，要求综合多个项目来源生成简洁且忠实的叙述。 - \*\*Circumstance Inference (CI, 20项)\*\*：评估对长程多方对话的理解与模式发现能力。智能体需识别符合特定情境的成员（如搭便车者、私下同步的协作者）。为此，论文专门修改基础工作区历史，将情境信号嵌入长程对话的行为模式中，而非集中于单条消息。 ### 3. 系统性实验与接口设计分析 论文对33个前沿模型与智能体harness进行大规模评估，开展超过40组实验，重点考察： - \*\*模型与Harness组合\*\*：测试8种harness（如Claude Code、Codex CLI、OpenHands、Antigravity等）与24种模型。 - \*\*数据访问接口\*\*：对比直接通过CLI访问导出的Slack工作区文件（使用\`grep\`、\`jq\`等终端工具）与通过MCP（Model Context Protocol）工具访问的效果。一个关键发现是，\*\*直接CLI访问平均提升7.85%的通过率，并显著降低成本与token消耗\*\*（如Claude Code配合Opus 5.5成本降低54%）。 - \*\*推理投入\*\*：验证更高推理努力（reasoning effort）通常带来性能提升。 ### 4. 失败模式深度分析 论文通过人工审查智能体轨迹，总结出若干反复出现的系统性弱点，为后续改进提供明确方向： - \*\*全局验证不足\*\*：智能体常在某频道找到局部 plausible 的答案后，未在更广的工作区上下文中验证，导致遗漏其他频道的后续反证。 - \*\*浅层代理推理\*\*：在缺乏直接证据时，智能体退而以消息量等浅层代理指标替代对实际工作的推理。 - \*\*错误前提识别失败\*\*：面对基于错误前提的查询，智能体常固守错误假设继续检索，最终产生幻觉答案。 - \*\*规划制品混淆\*\*：将讨论中的计划、TODO或大纲误认为已完成的事实，基于相关材料拼凑出看似合理但实际不存在的答案。 - \*\*关键词缺失即行为缺失\*\*：将Slack中缺乏字面关键词匹配等同于某行为未发生，忽视需要从间接信号推断的情境。 通过上述方法，SlackBench不仅提供了一个可扩展的评估框架，还揭示了当前前沿智能体在真实协作环境中仍存在显著的能力差距（最佳系统约80%，多数低于66%）。 Q4: 论文做了哪些实验？ 论文开展了以下系统性实验，涵盖主性能评测、接口设计消融、推理成本分析与可靠性验证等多个维度： ### 1. 主实验：前沿模型与 Harness 的综合性能评测 对 \*\*33 个前沿模型与 8 种 agent harness\*\* 在 SlackBench 的 140 个查询上进行了系统评估。实验设置统一为中等（medium）推理努力与 20 分钟时间限制。主要结果包括： - 9. MA-SafeDiffuser: Safe Multi-Agent Planning with Diffusion Probabilistic Models

Abstract:We propose MA-SafeDiffuser, a multi-agent extension of SafeDiffuser that equips diffusion-based trajectory planners with finite-time diffusion invariance guarantees for joint-agent safety specifications. 1. VAR-Q: Tuning-free KV Cache Quantization for Visual Autoregressive Image and Video Generation

Abstract:Visual autoregressive generation spans two dominant paradigms: next-scale prediction for images and video, and next-frame prediction for video. Both decode block-by-block rather than token-by-token, yielding KV caches whose statistics diverge from those of language models. At scale, KV memory exceeds the weights and limits large-scale deployment caused by a large amount of visual tokens. Our analysis of these caches reveals three findings. First, a small set of massive-value channels carries the functional load: ablating these outlier channels collapses generation, whereas ablating a matched set of inlier channels leaves quality intact. Second, the outliers organize along two axes, feature and block, with magnitudes persistent within each cell and shifting across cells. Third, this structure arises from two independent mechanisms: projection bias and RoPE-induced scattering. Guided by these findings, we propose VAR-Q, a tuning-free KV-cache quantization framework that groups along the same two axes. The generation loop provides block boundaries for free, removing the need for outlier detection or calibration. Paired with bit-packing kernels for runtime memory reduction, VAR-Q delivers low-bit quantization across nine models spanning both paradigms, cutting KV memory by up to 78.5% with negligible quality degradation and enabling 6x larger inference batches.

R(t to s)(z_t, x_0) = c_x z_t + c_0 x_0
其中
c_x = √{1 - barα_s1 - α_t}, quad c_0 = √α_s - c_x √α_t
**（2）PEEK 前缀缓存（PEEK Prefix Cache）** 一旦贪心搜索确定了位置 i 的门控 γ_i ，则 t(i+1) 时刻的状态即被确定且与后续门控无关。该状态被缓存下来，使得评估后续位置 j > i 的候选时，只需从该缓存状态开始计算后缀部分。 **成本降低效果**：以 N=9 步搜索为例，朴素无缓存搜索需要 225 次去噪器评估，而引入双缓存后降至 78 次，减少了 **65.3%** 的计算量。 —- ### 总结 PEEK 通过在选定步骤执行**单步前瞻、评估、插值**的闭环，将更高信噪比状态下的 cleaner signal 注入当前去噪步骤，从而在**不修改模型权重、不引入随机噪声、不改变时间网格**的前提下，系统性地修正了低 NFE 确定性采样中的暴露偏差。配合自动化调度搜索与缓存机制，该方法在部署时仅增加选定的若干次去噪器调用，即可在多个数据集和模型架构上实现可观的生成质量提升。 Q4: 论文做了哪些实验？ 论文在**像素空间与潜在空间**、**无条件/类别条件/文本条件**等多种设定下进行了系统评估，涵盖定量指标对比、消融分析与定性可视化。实验设置与结果可归纳如下。 —- ### 1. 实验设置 **数据集与分辨率** | 数据集 | 分辨率 | 生成类型 | 条件 | |:—-|:—-|:—-|:—-| | CIFAR-10 | 32 × 32 | 像素空间 | 无条件 | | LSUN Bedroom | 256 × 256 | 像素空间 | 无条件 | | ImageNet | 64 × 64 / 256 × 256 | 像素空间 / 潜在空间 | 类别条件 | | MS-COCO | 512 × 512 | 潜在空间 | 文本引导 | **模型架构** - CIFAR-10：DDPM 家族 UNet（Model A 与 Model B 两个检查点） - LSUN Bedroom：基础 DDPM 模型及 EDM 训练范式模型 - ImageNet 256×256：DiT-XL/2-256 潜在扩散 Transformer，classifier-free guidance scale = 4.0 - MS-COCO：Stable Diffusion v1.5，guidance scale = 7.5 - ImageNet-EDM 64×64 与 LSUN-EDM 256×256：基于 EDM 框架的训练模型 **求解器与基线** - 主实验使用 **DPM-Solver++**、**DEIS**、**UniPC**、**Heun’s 2nd** 等确定性一阶/高阶包装器 - **NFE-matched baseline**：以同等去噪器评估次数（NFE）运行相同求解器，但仅执行普通步骤、无前瞻插值 - **Restart-style repeat**：在同等 NFE 预算下执行“前向加噪—重新去噪”的随机校正机制，用于对比核心校正原理差异 - **iPNDM**：仅在 EDM LSUN 模型上作为额外基线 **评估指标** - **FID**（Fréchet Inception Distance）：越低越好；主报告使用 2048 维特征 - **PRDC**：Recall 与 Coverage（表 1） - **代理搜索**：CIFAR-10、ImageNet 256×256 与 MS-COCO 使用 256 维代理 FID 进行快速调度搜索，再以固定调度执行标准最终评估 —- ### 2. 计算基础干净预测：

x(0,i)^(base) = Dθ(z_i, t_i)

  • 构造试探性的临时下一状态：
    z(i+1) = Phi_i(z_i, x(0,i)^(base))
  • 在更高信噪比的临时状态上执行额外的去噪器查询，获得前瞻预测：
    x(0,i)^(look) = Dθ(z(i+1), t(i+1))
  • 通过门控 γi ∈ 0, 1 插值得到选定预测，并用于从原始状态 z_i 生成下一步：
    x    (0,i)^(sel) = (1-γi)x(0,i)^(base) + γix(0,i)^(look)

z(i+1)^(PEEK) = Phi_i(z_i, x(0,i)^(sel))

d_i^(sel) = (1-γ_i)d_i^(base) + γ_i d_i^(look)
其中 d_i^(base) 与 d_i^(look) 分别由当前状态与前瞻状态的模型输出经求解器特定转换得到。 —- ### 3. 插值调度搜索与缓存优化 由于并非每一步都值得消耗额外的去噪器评估，论文提出**贪心度量驱动搜索（greedy metric-driven search）**以自动确定门控调度 γ 。搜索遵循**NFE 匹配护栏（guardrail）**：仅当激活前瞻插值在验证指标（如 FID）上同时优于当前不激活方案以及将同等预算用于普通求解器步骤的基线时，才接受该位置的门控。 为降低一次性离线校准的计算成本，论文设计了两种缓存机制： - **基线噪声调度重基缓存（Baseline Noise-Schedule-Rebasing Cache）**：利用方差保持参数化下的状态重参数化公式

6. Augmentation with Reinforcement Transformation for Low-textured Scientific Sketch Image Retrieval

Abstract:Low-textured sketch images are widely used in scientific applications, including wildlife conservation, archaeological illustration, and forensic composites. Learning from and retrieving such images is challenging because they lack detailed surface textures and visual variations commonly exploited by modern deep learning models. This paper proposes Augmentation with Reinforcement Transformation (ART) to fine-tune pretrained models to learn effective representations for accurate sketch image retrieval. Our key observation is that sketches are particularly sensitive to image transformations, such as rotation, flipping, zooming, and stretching. While some transformations preserve their structural integrity, others may introduce distortions that hinder representation learning. Abstract:Point cloud denoising aims to recover clean geometry from noisy 3D observations while preserving both fine surface details and the underlying spatial distribution of the points. SurgTransfer 对此进行重新建模：将**初始帧** f0 作为唯一上下文，要求模型直接推断从 f_0 到未来整个片段 f(1:t) 的复合潜动作：

其中推断出的动作表示为序列形式：

9. SO(3)-Equivariant Learning on CAD Boundary Representations

Abstract:Learning on boundary representations (B-reps) requires robustness to the pose of a part, since CAD models are not stored in a canonical orientation. Existing B-rep networks either operate on world-frame coordinates and normals, and thus degrade under rotation, or encode each face in a local reference frame. Local frames yield invariance, but reduce inter-face messages to scalars and discard the relative orientation of adjacent faces. We propose SO3brep, an SO(3)-equivariant graph neural network on the B-rep face-adjacency graph. Node features are computed in local frames and mapped into a shared frame by Wigner D-matrices. Across three face-segmentation and two classification benchmarks with randomly rotated test parts, SO3brep achieves the highest accuracy among the compared B-rep networks and equivariant point cloud networks, with the fewest parameters. Its advantage is largest with few labeled parts, a setting typical of industrial CAD. Code is available at this https URL.

10. From Tokens to Trajectories: Parameter-Efficient Continuous Adaptation of Unified Multimodal Models

Abstract:While discrete tokenization in Unified Multimodal Models (UMMs) establishes strong semantic reasoning, it introduces a strict quantization bottleneck that degrades fine-grained visual details, often resulting in structurally accurate but artifact-heavy images.
