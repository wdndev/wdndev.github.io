1. ReCoPII: A Relation-Grounded Framework for Building Contextual PII Masking Datasets

Abstract:Training contextual masking systems for personally identifiable information (PII) requires examples distinguishing maskable from preservable mentions of one entity type, but privacy restrictions limit access to real data. Author-based augmentation (SPY) labels mentions unrelated to the author as non-PII, presuming a single data subject and overlooking others. We propose ReCoPII (Relation-grounded Contextual PII masking), a framework that builds such data without this premise: it plans ontology-constrained entity relations, writes insertion sentences with placeholders, reconstructs annotations with code, checks the result with a separate LLM, and derives MASK/KEEP labels from entity relations. On Korean documents from six domains, it yields ReCoPII-Ko, a corpus with relation, privacy, and MASK/KEEP annotations, which we validate. Across 300 pairs, four annotators agree with 88.42% of its sampled KEEP labels, against 26.27% for SPY-style augmentation of the same sources. Classifiers trained on it over-mask less than under that reference on a matched test. Contextual masking fine-tuned jointly with a PII detector removes most over-masking of detection alone within a fixed leakage budget; on human-verified entity records, the four detectors meeting it over-mask at most 5.04% with at most 4.18% leakage. The detector is comparable to XLM-R-large on KDPII with about a quarter of its parameters.

摘要：训练用于个人可识别信息（PII）的上下文掩码系统需要区分同一实体类型中可掩码与可保留的示例，但隐私限制限制了对真实数据的访问。基于作者的增强（SPY）将与作者无关的提及标记为非PII，假设只有单一数据主体并忽略其他人。我们提出了ReCoPII（基于关系的上下文PII掩码），这是一个无需此假设即可构建此类数据的框架：它规划本体约束的实体关系，使用占位符编写插入句子，用代码重建注释，通过单独的LLM检查结果，并从实体关系中推导MASK/KEEP标签。在来自六个领域的韩语文档上，它生成了ReCoPII-Ko，一个包含关系、隐私和MASK/KEEP注释的语料库，我们对其进行了验证。在300对样本中，四名标注者对其抽样的KEEP标签的一致率为88.42%，而对同来源的SPY风格增强仅为26.27%。在匹配测试上，基于它训练的分类器比参考方法掩码过度更少。与PII检测器联合微调的上下文掩码在固定泄露预算内去除了大部分单独检测的过度掩码；在人工验证的实体记录上，四个满足条件的检测器的过度掩码最多为5.04%，泄露最多为4.18%。该检测器在KDPII上的性能可与XLM-R-large相媲美，但参数约为其四分之一。

2. MedJev: Evaluating Zero-Shot System-One Decision Making in Medicine

Abstract:System One decision models such as Jev answer structured questions in a single forward pass without generating text, offering fast and computationally efficient clinical decision support. We evaluate MedJev, an open System One model that runs locally on a laptop, across ten medical benchmarks spanning medical knowledge, diagnosis, and patient level prediction using MIMIC IV and MIMIC IV ED. We compare MedJev against zero shot LLM baselines, published GPT results, and supervised models. MedJev achieves accuracies of 72.5 percent on MedQA, 75.4 percent on USMLE, and 80.1 percent on NEJM, approaching published GPT 4 performance while maintaining well calibrated confidence estimates (ECE 0.028 to 0.063). On patient level tasks, MedJev achieves an AUROC of 0.926 for In Hospital Mortality, exceeding XGBoost (0.902), but underperforms supervised models on operational outcomes such as ED Triage Admission (0.745 vs. 0.809). Patient level predictions also exhibit substantial miscalibration (In Hospital Mortality ECE 0.286). We introduce a lightweight, two parameter recalibration method that adapts predictions to local patient populations using 50 to 200 labeled examples, reducing ECE to 0.007. Our findings establish open System One models as a promising zero shot baseline for medical AI, combining competitive predictive performance with low computational cost and efficient local calibration. We release our code and evaluation harness to support future research.

3. OmniBrain-0: A Unified Agentic Model for Embodied Intelligence

Abstract:General-purpose embodied intelligence requires robots to coordinate diverse skills over long horizons, track task progress, and recover from failures. Specialist policies alone do not ensure task-level reliability, while powerful multimodal agents rely on costly reasoning and external experience that compact models struggle to reproduce. We introduce OmniBrain-0, a lightweight unified embodied agent that coordinates manipulation and navigation through a shared high-level decision model, integrating task understanding, planning, progress tracking, and feedback-driven recovery. Our progressive training framework combines embodied pretraining, domain-specialized agent distillation, and multi-teacher on-policy distillation (MOPD). It distills powerful teachers’ interaction experience into manipulation and navigation experts, then consolidates their decision capabilities into OmniBrain-0. Unification therefore occurs within the learned decision model rather than only at the tool interface. We further introduce Rubric-Guided Recursive Self-Improvement (RSI), a data flywheel in which a Meta-Agent turns execution evidence into refined rubrics, step-level reminders, and verified trajectories for domain-expert updates before their reconsolidation into OmniBrain-0 through MOPD. We evaluate OmniBrain-0 on LIBERO-Pro, LIBERO-Plus, RoboCasa365, RoboTwin 2.0, and R2R-CE Val-Unseen, as well as multiple real-world manipulation tasks. OmniBrain-0 combines strong cross-domain task performance with substantially lower inference and execution overhead than its closed-source teacher, demonstrating efficient transfer and consolidation of embodied capabilities into a compact unified agent.

4. FinLLM-KG: Knowledge Graph-Enhanced LLMs for Financial Sentiment Analysis in Algorithmic Trading

Abstract:Recent advances in Generative AI have substantially improved financial sentiment analysis through post-trained financial large language models (LLMs). However, existing approaches remain confined to predicting the sentiment of each article in isolation and operate as black boxes, which limits contextual understanding and hinders deployment in high-stakes financial decision-making. To this end, we introduce FinLLM-KG, a model-agnostic framework that combines causal LLMs with a financial knowledge graph, in order to inject temporally grounded, company-level knowledge from SEC 10-K filings at inference time, without updating model weights. Within FinLLM-KG, context is supplied either as a natural-language company profile or as a small set of relevance-ranked factual triples. This enables sentiment predictions to account for broader firm-level information rather than the article alone, at almost zero computational cost and for any causal LLM. In addition, attributing each article-level sentiment score to individual sentences and to the retrieved knowledge-graph facts makes the evidence behind the prediction inspectable, thereby enhancing explainability and supporting human-in-the-loop review. Simulations demonstrate that FinLLM-KG consistently improves portfolio performance over the state-of-the-art baseline.

5. 3, 7, 14, 17, 42: Instruction Tuning Installs and Sharpens Number-Choice Heuristics in Language Models

Abstract:We measure, without sampling error, the probability a language model assigns to each integer from 1 to N when asked for a random number. For a fixed prompt the model defines one probability vector, which we compute by summing over each integer’s scored spellings rather than estimate by sampling, removing the noise that limits earlier studies [1, 4]. We evaluate 18 open-weight model lines at five ranges up to N = 100, under 29 decoding configurations, on two inference stacks; 15 of them have matched base and instruction-tuned checkpoints that both give usable answers. Instruction tuning makes the distribution less uniform in all 15 pairs. It installs one heuristic, round-number avoidance, and sharpens another, endpoint avoidance. Base checkpoints do not avoid round numbers once the endpoints are removed from those sets; tuned checkpoints avoid them, multiples of 10 almost completely. Base checkpoints return the endpoints 1 and N at 0.30 to 0.60 of the interior rate; tuned checkpoints almost never return them. A preference for odd numbers is consistent across tuned checkpoints only at N = 100, where base checkpoints also show it weakly. Endpoint avoidance and the odd preference fit the account Kubovy and Psotka [6] proposed for human number choice; round-number avoidance is not part of that account, and the midpoint avoidance it predicts is essentially absent, with one slight exception at N = 100. Tuning does not install a new favourite integer shared across models: above N = 10 no modal integer is shared by more than 42 percent of tuned conditions, and P (17) rises in 10 of 15 pairs and falls in 5. The concentration is present in the model’s own distribution: at temperature 1.0, nucleus truncation at top-p 0.95 adds a median of 0.035 to the total variation distance.

6. Lipi-Ghor: A Large-Scale, Diarized, Multi-Domain Bengali Speech Corpus and a Scalable Pipeline for Building It

Abstract:Bengali, spoken by over 230 million people, remains severely under-resourced in spoken language research. Existing open Bengali ASR corpora are confined to short, read or crowd-sourced speech and lack speaker diarization and long-form audio. We present Lipi-Ghor, a large-scale weakly-supervised Bengali speech corpus comprising 885 hours of audio sourced from 998 YouTube videos across 596 unique channels, spanning 17 diverse domains including audiobooks, drama, religious sermons, news, and regional dialects. Each recording is annotated in SSTT format (Speaker, Speech, Transcription, Timestamp), which combines automated diarization with Bengali YouTube caption tracks. To our knowledge, Lipi-Ghor is the first Bengali speech corpus to pair large-scale coverage with speaker-diarized, long-form audio annotations, both of which are absent from existing open Bengali resources. We also present the construction methodology as a replicable pipeline for any language with a sufficient captioned YouTube presence. Targeted channel curation across a handful of rich domains yields corpus scales that were previously out of reach for low-resource languages. We benchmark four widely-used ASR models under zero-shot and fine-tuned conditions. All four degrade consistently on emotive, dialectal, and long-form content. Because caption targets are noisy, we also release a human-annotated gold evaluation split, verified by 17 volunteer annotators over 30 hours of human-in-the-loop correction and stratified across all 17 domains. We release our original contributions (video-ID lists, diarization output, pipeline code, and the gold annotations) under CC-BY-4.0, and distribute caption-derived transcripts for research use only; no raw audio is redistributed.

7. Frankly Speaking: The Company Election Integrity Keeps in Congressional Communication

Abstract:Election integrity is a shared commitment in American politics, but Democrats and Republicans in Congress increasingly mean different things by it. Analyzing 10,420 congressional e-newsletters sent from 2010 to 2025, we examine how the two parties invoke election terms such as integrity,''security,’’ and fraud.'' Using diachronic word embeddings, which track semantic change through how words are used in context, we measure how differently each party uses each term, how much each party shifts, and whether they share nearest semantic neighbors. We show that members of Congress do not need different words to tell different stories about elections. They need only place those same words in different company. Across nearly every term and year, the two parties surround election-integrity vocabulary with entirely different words. That divergence is easy to miss, because most election vocabulary is no more divergent than other words used at roughly the same frequency, and neither party's usage shifted more than the other's. Only a subset of terms grew more divergent, most notablycertification,’’ while rigged'' andstolen’’ entered election discourse after 2020 and polarized there. By 2024, Democrats surround voter,''election,’’ and integrity'' with the language of access and administration. Republicans surround the same terms withcitizenship verification,’’ noncitizen voting,'' andborder security.’’ For one party, integrity means protecting eligible voters’ ability to cast a ballot. Harness手动迭代无法匹配任务复杂度的增长 智能体性能日益受限于其控制框架（Harness）的组织与协调能力。随着任务多样性和长程交互复杂度的增加，依赖人工设计或逐任务调优 Harness 的方式已无法持续，亟需自动化的 Harness 进化机制。 ### 3. 现有RSI方法的跨任务泛化能力受限 当前 Harness RSI 研究主要聚焦于文本模态任务（如编程、数学），并采用\*\*任务特定进化\*\*（task-specific evolution）策略。这导致两个缺陷： - \*\*过拟合与碎片化\*\*：针对特定任务优化的 Harness 难以泛化到异构任务； - \*\*全局Harness的内在冲突\*\*：试图用一个通用 Harness 覆盖所有任务时，会面临不同任务间性能–效率权衡的冲突需求，降低适应效率。 ### 核心洞察与解决路径 论文提出，\*\*不同模态和表面形式的任务可能共享相同的基础能力需求\*\*（例如，ARC-AGI-3 和 Micromouse 都需要精确的感知与状态跟踪）。因此，问题应从"如何为每个任务优化 Harness"转变为：\*\*如何识别并利用任务间共享的能力需求，构建可复用的模块化 Harness，并通过自动化环境生成与验证实现持续的跨模态自我改进\*\*。 为此，论文提出 \*\*The Trinity\*\* 框架，通过 \*\*Creator\*\*（生成简化可验证的仿真世界）、\*\*Explorer\*\*（沿能力树进化可复用模块）和 \*\*Evolver\*\*（从验证轨迹中重组能力需求）三个角色，实现从"任务驱动"到"能力驱动"的递归自我改进。 Q2: 有哪些相关研究？ 该论文的相关研究主要围绕两大主线展开：\*\*递归 Harness 自我改进\*\*与\*\*智能体–环境协同进化\*\*。此外，引言部分还涉及多模态基础模型与交互智能体的基础支撑工作。具体分类如下： --- ### 1. 递归 Harness 自我改进（Recursive Harness Self-Improvement） 该领域研究如何在冻结模型权重（fixed backbone）的前提下，通过改进控制框架（Harness）实现智能体能力的持续提升与泛化： - \*\*自我改写与元优化\*\* - 早期理论工作如 Gödel machines

协同进化语言智能体与环境；Escher-Loop Q3: 论文如何解决这个问题？ 该论文通过引入 **The Trinity** 框架，将递归自我改进（RSI）的范式从“任务驱动”转向“**能力驱动**”，从而系统性地解决多模态智能体在规模化进化中面临的瓶颈。具体解决方案可从以下五个层面展开： —- ### 1. 核心范式：以共享能力需求重组任务空间 论文的核心洞察在于，**不同模态与表面形态的任务可能共享相同的基础能力需求**（例如，ARC-AGI-3 与 Micromouse 均依赖精确感知与状态跟踪）。因此，框架不再按任务类别或模态类型组织训练，而是通过分析验证轨迹，将任务按其**能力需求集合**重新组织： - 定义一组能力类别 K ，每个训练任务 τ 被赋予固定的需求集合 Kτ ⊂eq K 。 - 依据预设的能力顺序 prec 对 Kτ 排序，得到路径 pτ = sort_prec(Kτ) 。 - 通过 Trie 结构合并所有任务路径的公共前缀，构建出**层次化能力树**（Capability Tree）：

T = Triepτ : τ ∈ B(tr)
树中每个节点代表一种能力组合，节点上的训练任务共享该组合所要求的所有能力。 —- ### 2. 三角色协同闭环：Creator、Explorer、Evolver 框架通过三个角色的协同，将环境生成与 Harness 进化耦合在同一个能力树上： | 角色 | 核心职能 | 解决的关键问题 | |———|————-|———————| | **Evolver** | 从任务规范与验证轨迹中提取能力需求，构建并维护能力树；设计简化训练关卡 | 将异构任务映射到共享的能力维度，避免任务特定过拟合 | | **Creator** | 依据节点能力需求，生成简化、可验证的仿真世界（Capability-Driven Real2Sim2Real） | 降低大规模异构环境的构建成本，提供专注能力学习的课程 | | **Explorer** | 沿能力树执行训练关卡，通过选择性继承父节点模块并局部 refine，进化 Harness 树 | 实现模块化、可复用的 Harness 进化，支持跨任务泛化 | —- ### 3. 能力驱动的 Real2Sim2Real（Creator） 针对同一能力节点上的异构任务，Creator 将其**共同决策逻辑**抽象为简化的仿真训练关卡，而非直接复制原始任务： - **Sim 阶段**：基于节点能力需求、子节点信息及代表性任务的公开规范，Evolver 设计排除目标布局、私有证明与真实解迹的训练关卡；Creator 使用节点 Harness 实现这些关卡，并通过主机验证器池检查绑定、执行、状态转移与渲染的正确性。 - **Real 阶段**：训练完成后，直接在**原始基准任务**上评估性能，以衡量能力迁移效果。 这一机制使得智能体在紧凑的仿真课程中学习可迁移的底层决策能力，显著降低了训练环境数量与推理成本（实验表明，Capability Tree (Sim) 在仅使用 10 个 Harness 和 11 个环境的情况下，即达到最优的模块级性能）。 —- ### 4. Harness 树的选择性继承与局部细化（Explorer） Explorer 沿能力树构建与树结构对齐的 Harness 树，通过以下机制实现高效进化： - **节点状态**：节点 v 在第 i 次迭代后的 Harness 由继承模块 P(v)^((i)) 与局部模块 L(v)^((i)) 组成：

H(E,v)^((i)) = P(v)^((i)) oplus L(v)^((i))
其中 oplus 为右偏并集（本地模块优先覆盖同名继承模块）。 - **选择性继承**：子节点仅在其父节点更新后的版本在相同验证任务上取得**严格更高**的任务完成数时，才继承该更新；否则保留先前版本。这确保了祖先模块的稳定性与有效性。 - **持续细化**：Explorer 在准入的仿真课程上 rollout 收集轨迹 Z(v)^((i+1)) ，Evolver 据此对比成功与失败轨迹，提出局部模块更新：
L(v)^((i+1)) = Evolve(L(v)^((i)), Z(v)^((i+1)), F(v)^((i)))
主机验证证据、所有权、接口与版本谱系后，接受或拒绝更新。 - **Base Harness 整合**：每次迭代结束时，Creator 将所有节点验证通过的模块提案与当前 Base Harness 库合并，去重并保留互补功能，形成下一代共享 Base Harness H_(C)^((i+1)) 。 —- ### 5. 能力感知的路由（Capability-Aware Harness Routing） 由于进化后的 Harness 在不同能力分支上呈现专业化，框架引入路由机制为每个任务匹配兼容的模块配置： - 利用公开任务信息（目标、初始观测、元数据）与**已验证的执行经验**（存储于 FIFO 记忆池中），Router 检索相似历史记录并选择候选 Harness：

h_n = π(x_n, C, R(x_n, M_n), S(M_n))

  • 该机制在冻结的能力树与版本化 Harness 池上运行，无需访问未见任务的能力标签，即可实现任务到 Harness 的动态匹配，避免了单一全局 Harness 的内在冲突。 —- ### 总结 通过上述设计，The Trinity 将原本碎片化的任务特定优化，转化为在**固定拓扑的能力树上对可复用模块的递归进化**。Creator 提供低成本、高保真的仿真课程，Explorer 通过选择性继承累积验证有效的更新，Evolver 则确保整个系统始终围绕“共享能力需求”这一语义基础进行组织与改进，从而实现了跨模态、跨任务的 Harness 自我改进与泛化。 Q4: 论文做了哪些实验？ 论文在第4节（Experiments）中设计并执行了六组实验，系统验证能力驱动递归自我改进的有效性。实验基于 **28个训练基准** 与 **18个未见基准**（含两个真实世界具身任务），所有变体共享冻结的 Claude Opus 5 骨干与初始 Base Harness H0 。以下按实验目标分述： —- ### 1. 实验设置（Experimental Setup） - 4. CohortFlow: A Steerable Clinical Cohort Curation Agent with Localized Semantic Correction

Abstract:Language model agents for clinical cohort curation should accommodate evolving eligibility criteria without repeatedly processing unchanged requirements. 5. Why and How to Do Science When AI Is Better at It: A Framework Grounded in Human Understanding and Direction

Abstract:As AI begins to match or exceed human researchers at core scientific tasks, two questions become pressing: \emph{why} should humans keep doing science, and \emph{how} should they use AI when they do? It holds even if AI outperforms human researchers, rather than depending on AI’s current limitations. And it judges each use of AI by its effects on human understanding and direction rather than by the type of tool, across every phase of research, including peer review and the training of new researchers. It closes with the reforms needed to keep science aimed at human understanding and direction.

T = Triepτ : τ ∈ B(tr)
树中每个节点代表一种能力组合，节点上的训练任务共享该组合所要求的所有能力。 —- ### 2. 三角色协同闭环：Creator、Explorer、Evolver 框架通过三个角色的协同，将环境生成与 Harness 进化耦合在同一个能力树上： | 角色 | 核心职能 | 解决的关键问题 | |———|————-|———————| | **Evolver** | 从任务规范与验证轨迹中提取能力需求，构建并维护能力树；设计简化训练关卡 | 将异构任务映射到共享的能力维度，避免任务特定过拟合 | | **Creator** | 依据节点能力需求，生成简化、可验证的仿真世界（Capability-Driven Real2Sim2Real） | 降低大规模异构环境的构建成本，提供专注能力学习的课程 | | **Explorer** | 沿能力树执行训练关卡，通过选择性继承父节点模块并局部 refine，进化 Harness 树 | 实现模块化、可复用的 Harness 进化，支持跨任务泛化 | —- ### 3. 能力驱动的 Real2Sim2Real（Creator） 针对同一能力节点上的异构任务，Creator 将其**共同决策逻辑**抽象为简化的仿真训练关卡，而非直接复制原始任务： - **Sim 阶段**：基于节点能力需求、子节点信息及代表性任务的公开规范，Evolver 设计排除目标布局、私有证明与真实解迹的训练关卡；Creator 使用节点 Harness 实现这些关卡，并通过主机验证器池检查绑定、执行、状态转移与渲染的正确性。 - **Real 阶段**：训练完成后，直接在**原始基准任务**上评估性能，以衡量能力迁移效果。 这一机制使得智能体在紧凑的仿真课程中学习可迁移的底层决策能力，显著降低了训练环境数量与推理成本（实验表明，Capability Tree (Sim) 在仅使用 10 个 Harness 和 11 个环境的情况下，即达到最优的模块级性能）。 —- ### 4. Harness 树的选择性继承与局部细化（Explorer） Explorer 沿能力树构建与树结构对齐的 Harness 树，通过以下机制实现高效进化： - **节点状态**：节点 v 在第 i 次迭代后的 Harness 由继承模块 P(v)^((i)) 与局部模块 L(v)^((i)) 组成：

H(E,v)^((i)) = P(v)^((i)) oplus L(v)^((i))
其中 oplus 为右偏并集（本地模块优先覆盖同名继承模块）。 - **选择性继承**：子节点仅在其父节点更新后的版本在相同验证任务上取得**严格更高**的任务完成数时，才继承该更新；否则保留先前版本。这确保了祖先模块的稳定性与有效性。 - **持续细化**：Explorer 在准入的仿真课程上 rollout 收集轨迹 Z(v)^((i+1)) ，Evolver 据此对比成功与失败轨迹，提出局部模块更新：
L(v)^((i+1)) = Evolve(L(v)^((i)), Z(v)^((i+1)), F(v)^((i)))
主机验证证据、所有权、接口与版本谱系后，接受或拒绝更新。 - **Base Harness 整合**：每次迭代结束时，Creator 将所有节点验证通过的模块提案与当前 Base Harness 库合并，去重并保留互补功能，形成下一代共享 Base Harness H_(C)^((i+1)) 。 —- ### 5. 能力感知的路由（Capability-Aware Harness Routing） 由于进化后的 Harness 在不同能力分支上呈现专业化，框架引入路由机制为每个任务匹配兼容的模块配置： - 利用公开任务信息（目标、初始观测、元数据）与**已验证的执行经验**（存储于 FIFO 记忆池中），Router 检索相似历史记录并选择候选 Harness：

h_n = π(x_n, C, R(x_n, M_n), S(M_n))

  • 该机制在冻结的能力树与版本化 Harness 池上运行，无需访问未见任务的能力标签，即可实现任务到 Harness 的动态匹配，避免了单一全局 Harness 的内在冲突。 —- ### 总结 通过上述设计，The Trinity 将原本碎片化的任务特定优化，转化为在**固定拓扑的能力树上对可复用模块的递归进化**。Creator 提供低成本、高保真的仿真课程，Explorer 通过选择性继承累积验证有效的更新，Evolver 则确保整个系统始终围绕“共享能力需求”这一语义基础进行组织与改进，从而实现了跨模态、跨任务的 Harness 自我改进与泛化。 Q4: 论文做了哪些实验？ 论文在第4节（Experiments）中设计并执行了六组实验，系统验证能力驱动递归自我改进的有效性。实验基于 **28个训练基准** 与 **18个未见基准**（含两个真实世界具身任务），所有变体共享冻结的 Claude Opus 5 骨干与初始 Base Harness H0 。以下按实验目标分述： —- ### 1. 实验设置（Experimental Setup） - 4. CohortFlow: A Steerable Clinical Cohort Curation Agent with Localized Semantic Correction

Abstract:Language model agents for clinical cohort curation should accommodate evolving eligibility criteria without repeatedly processing unchanged requirements. 5. Why and How to Do Science When AI Is Better at It: A Framework Grounded in Human Understanding and Direction

Abstract:As AI begins to match or exceed human researchers at core scientific tasks, two questions become pressing: \emph{why} should humans keep doing science, and \emph{how} should they use AI when they do? It holds that one indispensable aim of science is \emph{durable, collective human understanding of the world, pursued in service of human ends and values}. From this principle it derives a two-part test for when a research task can be delegated to AI. The first part, \emph{human understanding}, requires that the scientific community remain able to explain, defend and contest the resulting claims, and retain the skills to do so. The framework differs from existing guidance in three ways. It rests on an account of what science is for rather than on integrity norms. It holds even if AI outperforms human researchers, rather than depending on AI’s current limitations. And it judges each use of AI by its effects on human understanding and direction rather than by the type of tool, across every phase of research, including peer review and the training of new researchers. It closes with the reforms needed to keep science aimed at human understanding and direction.

8. Learning to Read Braille: Benchmarking and Improving Braille Recognition Capabilities of VLMs

Abstract:Despite rapid progress in visual understanding and document transcription, modern vision-language models (VLMs) struggle with a fundamental accessibility task: reading Braille from images. We introduce BrailleBench, a manually curated benchmark of 259 real-world Grade-1 and Grade-2 Braille images. We evaluate proprietary, open-weight, OCR-specialized, and tool-assisted multimodal systems and find that standalone general-purpose models perform poorly at directly transcribing Braille images, whereas tool-assisted systems perform much better. We design a synthetic Braille generation pipeline with diverse layouts and image perturbations. A 2B VLM model trained on our synthetic data reaches 87% exact match on Grade-1 and 61% on Grade-2 BrailleBench, outperforming standalone VLMs and achieving the highest Grade-2 exact match score among the evaluated systems. We further show that high accuracy on synthetic validation data does not guarantee high accuracy on real photographs in our experiments: models trained on prior synthetic data reach up to 96% on their synthetic validation set but at most 3.26% on Grade-1 BrailleBench. Our results identify an accessibility-relevant capability gap in multimodal systems and show that targeted synthetic supervision can improve real-world Braille transcription.

9. UT-HMR: Uncertainty-Guided Temporal Modeling for Robust 3D Human Mesh Recovery

Abstract:Monocular 3D human mesh recovery (HMR) remains unreliable under weak observations caused by long camera-to-person distances, occlusion, and motion blur. 10. Diagnosing and Correcting Bias in MLLM for Long Video Understanding

Abstract:Long-video question answering is challenging since the answer often hinges on a few decisive moments scattered throughout the video, while memory constraints force Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) to sample frames at extremely low rates, resulting in sparsity that risks omitting key evidence. Though aggressively enlarging the context size is intuitive, we argue that performance remains fundamentally constrained by the bias, \emph{i.e.}, the misleading cues caused by spurious \textit{linguistic} correlations and salient yet irrelevant \textit{visual} observations. In this paper, we first introduce {S-MME}, a benchmark designed to systematically diagnose shortcut-biased behavior in MLLMs. To mitigate this failure, we further propose a training-free framework, Counterfactual Long-video Evidence-Aware Reasoning (CLEAR). By treating the full video-question pair as a complete view, CLEAR estimates bias effects via augmented views by only keeping the question or locally salient visual clips. Then, we intervene hidden states to mitigate the discrepancy at inference such that the correct answer can be predicted confidently. With experimental studies across five benchmarks, we show that our method is generalizable and can be integrated in different MLLMs, yielding consistent gains in prediction and robustness. We hope CLEAR can offer a complementary direction in test-time scaling for long-video understanding.
